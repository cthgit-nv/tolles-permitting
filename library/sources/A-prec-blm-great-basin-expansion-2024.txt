Project Description
The staff of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC or Commission) has prepared an environmental assessment (EA) for the 2024 Great Basin Expansion Project (Project), proposed by Great Basin Gas Transmission Company (Great Basin) in the above-referenced docket. Great Basin requests authorization to construct, operate, and abandon certain natural gas pipeline facilities. The expansion would provide 5,674 dekatherms per day of incremental firm transportation service for two existing firm transportation shippers and would require construction of approximately 3.4 miles of upsized or looped pipeline in three segments across Douglas, Lyon, and Storey Counties, Nevada. 
The Bureau of Land Management (BLM) participated as a cooperating agency in the preparation of the EA. The BLM will adopt and use the EA to consider the issuance of a right-of-way grant for the portion of the project on federal lands.

INTRODUCTION 
On May 1, 2023, Great Basin Gas Transmission Company (Great Basin) filed an 
application to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) under sections 7(b) and (c) of 
the Natural Gas Act (NGA) and Part 157 of the Commission’s regulations for authorization to 
abandon existing facilities and to construct, and operate certain pipeline facilities in Douglas, 
Lyon, and Storey Counties, Nevada (2024 Expansion Project). The project is designed to enable 
Great Basin to provide an additional 5,674 dekatherms per day (Dth/d) of incremental firm 
transportation service for two local distribution companies. 
 
The Bureau of Land Management (BLM), Sierra Front Field Office (SFFO) received 
applications on January 13th and February 1st of 2023 for temporary use areas (BLM Serial 
Number NVN-0060169-04) associated with the 2024 Great Basin Expansion Project. Great 
Basin proposed to utilize (3) 220’ x 35’ turn out areas along existing roads and (1) 350’ x 30’ 
staging area on public lands.  
 
On February 1, 2023, the BLM, SFFO, also received an application for an amendment to an 
existing right-of-way authorization held by Great Basin.  Great Basin proposed to install a 20” 
natural gas pipeline withing the existing authorization (BLM Serial Number NVN-0060169) to 
increase the capacity to serve the surrounding communities.   
 
Great Basin, a Nevada corporation, is a natural gas company, engaged in the transportation and 
storage of natural gas in interstate commerce subject to the FERC’s jurisdiction. Great Basin’s 
interstate natural gas system extends from the Idaho-Nevada border to the California-Nevada 
state line near Lake Tahoe. 
 
The proposed project will enable Great Basin to provide 5,674 Dth per day of incremental firm 
transportation service on its mainline system to points downstream of Wadsworth, Nevada, to 
meet the incremental service requirements of two existing firm transportation shippers, 
Southwest Gas Corporation – Northern Nevada (Southwest Gas Nevada) and Southwest Gas 
Corporation – Northern California (Southwest Gas California) (together Southwest Gas), both of 
which are local distribution companies, and to accommodate Southwest Gas Nevada’s request to 
shift delivery point rights. Southwest Gas Nevada and Southwest Gas California are affiliates of 
Great Basin through their parent company. 
 
Great Basin seeks authorization to construct, abandon, or replace the following natural gas 
facilities on lands managed by the BLM: 
 
• Silver Springs Segment – Abandon in place or by removal approximately 2.88 
miles of 10-inch diameter Carson Lateral pipeline between mileposts CA-15.85

PUBLIC INVOLVEMENT 
• Notice of Great Basin’s application was published in the Federal Register on May 19, 
2023.  
• On June 15, 2023, FERC issued a Notice of Scoping Period requesting comments on 
environmental issues for the proposed Project. The notice was published in the Federal 
Register and mailed to interested parties, including federal, state, and local government 
representatives and agencies; elected officials; environmental and public interest groups; 
Native American Tribes; other interested parties; and local libraries and newspapers. 
Publication of the NOS established a 30-day public comment period for submission of 
comments, concerns, and issues related to the environmental aspects of the Project. All 
comments received by FERC are part of the public record for the Project and are 
available for viewing on the FERC website (www.FERC.gov). 
• In response to the NOS, FERC received comments from Southwest Gas Corporation, the 
Office of the Nevada Attorney General, Bureau of Consumer Protection, the Department 
of Conservation and Natural Resources, and the Nevada State Historic Preservation 
Office (SHPO). The primary issues raised by the commentors included concerns about 
the purpose and need and precedent agreements, the source of water for construction, and 
remaining cultural surveys required for compliance under Section 106 of the NHPA. The 
Commission developed a “Certificate Policy Statement” that established criteria for 
determining whether there is a need for a proposed project and whether the proposed 
project would serve the public interest. The Commission decision, in its Order, would 
review the need for the Project. All substantive environmental comments are addressed 
throughout the EA. 
• On July 10, 2023, the Commission issued a Notice of Schedule for the Preparation 
of an Environmental Assessment for the 2024 Expansion Project. The notice was 
mailed to interested parties, including federal, state, and local officials; agency 
representatives; environmental and public interest groups; Native American Tribes; local 
libraries and newspapers; and affected property owners. 
• On November 28, 2023, The Environmental Assessment (EA) was issued for a 30-day 
public comment period.  A Notice of Availability of the EA was published in the Federal 
Register and was mailed to interested parties, including federal, state, and local officials; 
agency representatives; environmental and public interest groups; Native American 
Tribes; local libraries and newspapers; and affected property owners. 
 
DECISION 
Based on the analysis in the 2024 Great Basin Gas Expansion Project, EA# DOI-BLM-NV-
C020-2024-0028-EA and the associated Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI), it is my 
decision to approve the application for two temporary use areas (BLM Serial Number NVN-
0060169-04) and the application to amend the existing Rights of Way (BLM Serial Number 
NVN-0060169). This decision will allow Great Basin to implement its Proposed Action 
Alternative as described in the EA with the identified Best Management Practices (BMPs) and 
mitigation measures as identified in Section B of the EA document.

This decision is effective upon issuance in accordance with Title 43 of the Code of Federal 
Regulations (CFR) at §2801.10. This determination is in conformance with NEPA as amended 
(72 USC 4321 et.seq.); the 2001 Carson City Field Office Consolidated Resource Management 
Plan, and current BLM policies, plans and programs. 
 
RATIONALE FOR DECISION 
Upon analyzing the impacts of the Proposed Action Alternative and following issuance of the 
EA for public review, I have determined that implementing the Proposed Action Alternative as 
described in Section A of the EA with Best Management Plans identified in the SWPPP, the 
Noxious and Invasive Weed Control Plan, and the Restoration and Revegetation Plan, will not 
have a significant impact to the human environment and that an EIS is not required (FONSI pp. 
1-4).  
 
AUTHORITIES 
The Proposed Action is in conformance with the FLPMA of 1976 as amended, Title V Sec. 501 
Grant, issue, or renewal of rights-of-way, , the regulations at Title 43 of the CFR §2800 and 
§2900, the 2001 Carson City Field Office Consolidated Resource Management Plan, and with 
current BLM policies, plans and programs. The Proposed Action is consistent in relationship to 
statutes, regulations and policies of neighboring local, County, State, Tribal governments and 
other Federal agencies. 
 
The Best Management Plans identified in the SWPPP, the Noxious and Invasive Weed Control 
Plan, and the Restoration and Revegetation Plan and the FONSI show that all practicable means 
to avoid or minimize environmental harm have been adopted and that unnecessary or undue 
degradation of the public lands will not occur as a result of the proposed Great Basin Gas 
Pipeline Expansion project.
