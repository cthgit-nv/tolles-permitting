Proposed Action 
NV Energy proposes to use the identified area (Exhibit A) as a helicopter landing site 
and staging area during the rebuild of three existing 120kV electric transmission lines. 
No permanent structures or facilities will be constructed within the staging areas. The 
areas will be used to support the Verdi to Donner Summit (101‐102‐133) Transmission 
Line Rebuild Project (Project) in Sierra and Nevada Counties, California. Power line 
towers will be constructed for transport to locations along the power line rights-of-way. 
The staging areas will also be used to store insulat ors, shield wire, and aluminum 
conductors. Construction vehicles and equipment will be used on the sites. NV Energy is 
requesting a 5-year use authorization for construction and restoration, beginning in 
Spring of 2026.

Proposed Action 
The Bureau of Reclamation (Reclamation) is issuing a 25-year license to the City of Fernley 
(Fernley), Nevada, for an existing sewage effluent pipeline and cascade aerator/outfall structure 
which are located on Reclamation first form withdrawn land for the Newlands Project (NW¼, 
NE¼ Section 16, Township 20 North, Range 25 East, Lyon County, Nevada).  Reclamation’s 
license will provide Fernley with continued use of a 50-foot-wide pipeline corridor (1.025 acres) and 
a 150-foot by 150-foot outfall structure area (0.5165 acre).  The pipeline consists of 1,300 feet of 
buried 12-inch-diameter polyethylene pipe.  The concrete outfall structure is 32 feet long by 8 feet 
wide.  These facilities are used to transport and oxygenate treated sewage effluent from Fernley’s 
East Wastewater Treatment Plant (Plant) across Reclamation land and discharge it into a natural 
drainage flowing into the Fernley Wildlife Management Area (WMA).  The Plant has a maximum 
capacity of 3 million gallons per day.  Fernley has a Clean Water Act Section 402 National Pollutant 
Discharge Elimination System permit and complies with State of Nevada Water Pollution Control 
Law (NRS 445A.300-445A.730).  Fernley also has an agreement with the Nevada Department of 
Wildlife for the effluent discharge into the Fernley WMA.  Fernley performs minimal routine 
maintenance on the pipeline and outfall structure.  No new construction or ground disturbance is 
proposed.  Their current Reclamation license will expire in June 2022.

Proposed Action 
The proposed action is to install a steel footbridge over the Truckee Canal to facilitate 
the deployment of water measurement devices and provide access for discharge 
measurements of the Truckee Canal. This structure will improve safety for Reclamation 
staff conducting field activities necessary for the operation of the canal.  
 
This minor construction activity consists primarily of installing a 3 -feet by 2-feet by 1.5-
feet pre-cast concrete footing on the west side of the canal prism and a small section of 
angle iron on the east side of the canal prism. The footbridge will be ins talled 
approximately 5 feet above the high -water mark.  
 
The installation of the pre-cast concrete footing within the west prism is not expected to 
require any excavation. A long-reach excavator bucket will make an imprint in the soil to 
place the footing. A small piece of angle iron will be anchored on the east  side of the 
concrete liner to secure that end of the bridge.  
 
Access to the ends of the bridge for both installation and use will be via existing access 
roads. The east side access road is paved as it is also access to Lahontan Dam. The west 
side access road is an existing gated dirt road.  
 
Equipment to move the bridge onto the footings will be staged on an existing dirt lot. 
The excavator will be used to pick the bridge off the transport via the existing paved 
access road.  
 
The steel bridge is constructed from bomb boxes supplied by the U.S. Navy. The bridge 
spans 36.5 feet. It will be bolted to the concrete footing and angle iron.  
 
A steel ladder with handrails will be doweled into the existing concrete liner and 
attached with expansion anchors on the east side of the canal. It will also be attached to 
the footbridge, allowing for safe access and escape.  
Exclusion Category 
DOI NEPA Handbook, 516 DM 1, Appendix 2, Bureau of Reclamation, 14.5 C(3) – Minor 
construction activities associated with authorized projects which correct environmental 
conditions or which merely augment or supplement, or are enclosed within existing 
facilities.
