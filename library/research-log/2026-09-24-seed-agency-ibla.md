Key findings:

- The Reclamation CE is now DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, 14.5(D)(8). Its text is the January 13, 2025 revision word for word, with four prongs joined by "one or more". Old D(10) (crossing agreements) was folded into D(8) and is now Reserved.
- BLM Realty CE numbering E(1) to E(20) is unchanged in Handbook App. 2, 11.9(E).
- BLM adopted TVA n.16 (new transmission lines of about 10 miles or less and 125 acres or less of ROW; substations of 10 acres or less), RUS 1970.54(c)(2), DOE B4.11 and USACE 230.9(i) in 2025. They are listed at Handbook App. 2, 16.1(E).
- IBLA standing moved from 43 C.F.R. 4.410 to 4.402 (and 4.401 definitions) effective Feb. 10, 2025.
- Carson City District and LBAO have used a single BLM-lead EA with Reclamation adoption for a linear utility through Churchill County (Vero fiber, 2024).

## Not found

- **No BLM Realty CE (Handbook App. 2, 11.9(E)) covers a new stand-alone transmission line, substation or access road on undisturbed land.** Checked the full E(1) to E(20) list in the February 2026 Handbook (pp. 70-71). The only BLM-usable CEs that reach new transmission lines and substations are adopted ones at 16.1(E)(17), (20), (22) (pp. 108-109).
- **No BLM Nevada decision applying the 2025 adopted TVA n.16, RUS 1970.54(c)(2) or DOE B4.11 CEs** was found. Searched the NEPA Register with keywords transmission, substation, "power line", powerline, NV Energy, Gridliance, Greenlink, Harry Allen, Eldorado and Silver Springs, and read the Carson City District and Southern Nevada District result lists. The 2026 Carson City District transmission decision found (Silver Springs 345-kV fold) used an EA.
- **No Stillwater FO transmission, road or pipeline ROW EA near Hazen, Fallon or Lahontan Valley** was found on the Register. Stillwater results were renewals (CX), geothermal EAs (Patua Phase II, FORGE), communication sites, and one 2026 E(12) amendment CE. The Vero fiber EA is the only Stillwater-signed linear ROW EA found.
- **No Great Basin Gas Transmission ROW decision in Stillwater FO / Churchill County.** Found only the 2024 Sierra Front FO expansion (carded), a 2022 Tuscarora FO amendment CX, and FERC notices for the 2026 Expansion, the Gabbs Lateral NASF Relocation, and the 2028 Expansion (PF26-5, pre-filing).
- **No programmatic corridor EA for I-15 / Jean / Primm / Ivanpah Valley** was found on the Register (searched Jean, Primm, Ivanpah, "Ivanpah Valley", Stateline, Roach, Goodsprings). The Eldorado Valley programmatic EA (2012) does not reach Jean.
- **No Jean-area substation, transmission or fiber ROW NEPA document** was found in the LVFO results. Jean results were recreation events, a water well site EA (2019), a communication site EA (2019), mineral pits and staging areas.
- **No LBAO CEC or EA for a new paved public road on Newlands Project land or for a multi-utility crossing bundle.** Read all 131 CEC titles in the 2011-2026 LBAO annual lists and the 57 LBAO database entries. Only existing roads and facilities, single crossings, bridges and temporary uses were found.
- **No IBLA decision reviewing a BLM CX for a new transmission line, substation or utility ROW.** Searched the text of 479 downloaded decisions (1995-2026) for "categorical exclusion" together with right-of-way. Only ONDA (174 IBLA 26, short-term met towers, E(19)) was on point. The download set covered only decisions whose appellant names matched groups, utilities, counties and energy terms, so decisions under individuals' names were not searched.
- **No IBLA decision reviewing a Reclamation Part 429 use authorization on NEPA grounds** was found in the same corpus.
- **No current BLM-specific policy on applicant-prepared EAs under 43 C.F.R. 46.107(h)** was found on BLM's IM/PIM listings (FY2023-FY2026 pages checked) or by web search. IM 2006-011 expired in 2007.
- **No rescission or replacement of PIM 2018-023** found on BLM's IM/PIM listing pages.
- **No Reclamation-specific third-party or applicant-prepared EA guidance** found on usbr.gov/nepa or the Region 10 NEPA pages.
- regulations.gov copies of the Handbook appendices (DOI-2025-0004-0004, -0006) returned 403 and were not used.
