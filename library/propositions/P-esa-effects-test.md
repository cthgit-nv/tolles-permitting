## Why it matters to us

ESA scope uses a but-for test that NEPA no longer does. Private generation could be an
"effect of the action" for Section 7 even when outside NEPA scope, if it would not occur
but for the ROW and is reasonably certain. Denim: desert tortoise.

## Best supporting authority

50 C.F.R. 402.02. **Correction:** the Aug 14 memo (Q5) cites 402.17 as current; it was
removed May 6, 2024 (89 FR 24268). A November 2025 proposal would reinstate it (not final).

## Limits

The Part 402 consultation rules have a pending proposed rewrite (Nov. 2025). Watchlist.
