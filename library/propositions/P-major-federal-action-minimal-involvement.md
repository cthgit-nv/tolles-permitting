## Why it matters to us

Statutory backstop for keeping private generation and the Denim leasehold facilities out
of federal review, independent of any Interior procedure that could be vacated.

## Best supporting authority

42 U.S.C. 4336e(10)(B)(i) (A-usc-42-4336e). Two alternatives: minimal funding, or minimal
involvement where the agency cannot control the outcome.

## Limits

The statutory text is settled; applying it to our private facilities is advocacy, and
it applies to the private facilities, not to the ROW grants themselves, which are plainly
federal actions.
