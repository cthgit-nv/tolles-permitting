## Why it matters to us

The FMDP and Denim FONSIs will lean on design commitments (corridor sharing, tortoise
measures). Each commitment needs evidence it works, in the EA.

## Best supporting authority

166 IBLA 140 (2005) (A-ibla-suwa-wide-hollow, adverse on its facts). 174 IBLA 93 (2008)
(A-ibla-santa-fe-nw-info-council) upheld an access-road and utility ROW EA/FONSI where
private development was speculative and outside BLM's control.
