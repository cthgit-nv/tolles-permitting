## Why it matters to us

FMDP shares a corridor with the Great Basin Gas Transmission pipeline (fmdp facts F2).
Supports reviewing them separately, with the pipeline as a cumulative action.

## Best supporting authority

IBLA, 181 IBLA 325 (2012), a Nevada utility ROW case (A-ibla-cbd-lincoln-county-row).
152 IBLA 1 (2000) (A-ibla-defenders-wildlife-ajo): a mine was not an effect of a
230 kV line because it would reopen without it.

## Limits

IBLA is persuasive, not binding on courts. The pipeline may be Great Basin's 2028
Expansion (FERC PF26-5, BLM a planned cooperating agency); unconfirmed
(`Q-20260924-gbgt-pipeline-status`). If BLM cooperates on the pipeline's FERC review,
coordinate so the two records describe each other consistently.

## Relied on by

FMDP pathway; cumulative effects.
