## Why it matters to us

Keeps private-land generation (Hazen solar, BESS, geothermal, gas plant, data center)
and the Denim leasehold facilities out of the proposed action in each federal review.

## Best supporting authority

Seven County, 605 U.S. at 190 and 192 (A-case-seven-county): no duty to assess separate
projects because of but-for dependence or foreseeability; substantial deference on scope.
Ninth Circuit applied it in Arizona Mining Reform (A-case-az-mining-reform, amended Apr.
2026, preliminary injunction posture).

## Best adverse authority and the answer to it

Save Our Sonoran (A-case-save-our-sonoran, questioned) required the Corps to analyze the
whole development. Answer: pre-Seven County; it rested on the Corps' permit being a
precondition to the whole project on jurisdictional washes, and on a scope rule Seven
County's deference displaces. Border Power (questioned) used but-for causation, which
Public Citizen and Seven County reject.

## Limits

- Deference is to the agency's line. The agency must draw it; we can only make it easy
  to draw. The record must show the separate projects are separate.
- Does not control ESA Section 7 or NHPA Section 106 scope (see P-esa-effects-test,
  P-section-106-ape-indirect-effects).
- If the private project needs its own federal permits, those permits get their own review.
