## Why it matters to us

BLM and Reclamation have no authority over generation, substations or data centers on
private or State land. This is the strongest single reason those facilities stay out.

## Best supporting authority

Public Citizen, 541 U.S. at 770 (A-case-public-citizen), quoted in Seven County, 605 U.S.
at 188. Cumberland (D.C. Cir. 2025) applied it to a pipeline and a power plant FERC could
not regulate (two-judge majority on this point).

## Best adverse authority and the answer to it

None squarely contrary after 2025. Watch for arguments that a ROW condition (for example,
limits on what the line may carry) gives the agency some control.

## Limits

Does not help where the agency does have authority over both actions. **It does not help
the Application 1 vs Application 2 question**: Reclamation decides both. Use
P-segmentation-independent-utility there.
