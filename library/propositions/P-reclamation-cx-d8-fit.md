## Why it matters to us

The Application 1 pathway (Aug 14 memo Q7; `Q-20260924-cx-fit-road-and-bundle`).

## Best supporting authority

DOI NEPA Handbook, App. 2, 14.5(D)(8) (A-guid-bor-cx-d8). The text is settled: prongs are
joined by "one or more", so prong (a) alone suffices, and the old crossing-agreement CE
(D(10)) was folded into D(8) in January 2025. Lahontan Basin Area Office has applied the
revised D(8) to an NV Energy use authorization (A-prec-bor-lbao-nv-energy-hlz-cec-2026,
date unverified). LBAO previously licensed a road near Fallon that allows future
utilities under the old D(10) (Marburg Technology Road License CEC, research log
2026-09-24-seed-agency-ibla).

## Best adverse authority and the answer to it

- No LBAO CEC found for a **new paved road** or a **bundle of new utility crossings**
  (research log, Not found). For a new substation on Reclamation land in Churchill County
  (Lahontan Substation, 2014), LBAO used an EA.
- Prong (b) ("does not lead to a major public or private action") invites the argument
  that the crossings enable CHIP. Answer: the prongs are disjunctive; rely on (a). But
  do not concede (b) in the record; a CHIP-enabling framing also feeds the segmentation
  argument (P-segmentation-independent-utility).
- Extraordinary circumstances (43 C.F.R. 46.215) can defeat any CX: listed species,
  historic properties, controversy, connected actions with significant effects.

## Limits

Status is **open** as applied: the text supports us, but no precedent matches a 2,750-foot
paved road plus water, wastewater, overhead, MV and fiber. Sequencing (road first,
utilities later) stays the fallback. Handbook status follows the challenge to Interior's
procedures (A-guid-doi-nepa-handbook).
