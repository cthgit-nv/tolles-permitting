## Why it matters to us

The practical lever for both Hazen scoping questions: we want each agency to draw the
line where we would, and to write a record that shows why.

## Best supporting authority

Seven County, 605 U.S. at 182 and 192 (manageable line; substantial deference). Save the
Sound applies it to project definition (D.C. Cir., persuasive). Cumberland: "the era of
searching NEPA review is over" (two-judge majority; persuasive).

## Best adverse authority and the answer to it

Sovereign Inupiat: deference also protects an agency that draws the line broadly.
Answer: not adverse on the law, adverse in practice. Win the scoping conversation with
BLM and Reclamation early; deference follows the agency, not the applicant.

## Limits

Most post-Seven County appellate authority on project definition is D.C. Circuit. Ninth
Circuit authority so far (Arizona Mining Reform) concerns separate non-federal projects,
not two federal actions.
