## What it is

The Clark County MSHCP is the habitat conservation plan behind the section 10(a)(1)(B) incidental take permit held by Clark County, the Cities of Las Vegas, North Las Vegas, Henderson, Mesquite and Boulder City, and NDOT. It covers the desert tortoise and other covered species. `decided` is set to the plan's "9/00" date (September 2000; exact day not stated). The permit number and issuance date were not read from the permit itself.

## Operative text or holding

- Coverage is limited to non-Federal land. The plan provides section 10(a) coverage on non-Federal lands in Clark County. It "will not provide for incidental take on Federal lands or resulting from Federal actions on non-Federal lands". For federal lands and federal actions it supplies only a framework for avoidance and minimization under section 7.
- Covered land uses on non-Federal lands include utilities, transportation, and residential, commercial and industrial development. Total take is capped at 145,000 acres.
- The County's "DCP and MSHCP 101" deck describes the scope as all non-Federal (private, municipal, state) lands in Clark County, plus NDOT activities south of the 38th parallel below 5,000 feet. It gives a 30-year permit starting February 2001. Funding includes "Section 10 mitigation fees" and "Section 7 mitigation fees".
- Amendment: the County's FAQ says the permit expires when all 145,000 acres are developed or in January 2031, whichever comes first. About 17,000 acres remained. The County plans to apply to amend the permit: more acres, a revised species list and a 50-year term.

## Quotes

> "Although the MSHCP will not provide for incidental take on Federal lands" (MSHCP ch. 2, at 2-3)
> "or resulting from Federal actions on non-Federal lands, it does provide the framework for avoidance and minimization of impacts to Covered Species on Federal lands" (MSHCP ch. 2, at 2-4)
> "In no event, however, shall the total amount of take exceed 145,000 acres." (MSHCP ch. 2, sec. 2.1.4, at 2-4)
> "The original permit is set to expire when all 145,000 acres has been developed, or in January of 2031, whichever comes first." (Clark County MSHCP Permit Amendment FAQ)

## Treatment

2026-09-24: the plan chapter (Sept. 2000) and the County's current amendment FAQ page were read. The permit document and any FWS amendment decision were not read. The FAQ does not say whether the amendment application has been filed or approved.

## Notes

- Jean: the BLM ROW (substation, lines, road and fiber on BLM land) gets no MSHCP take coverage. Tortoise take there runs through section 7 and the BLM SNDO PBO (see A-guid-tortoise-programmatic-bo).
- The facilities on the Nevada Division of State Lands leasehold are on non-Federal land. The County deck lists state land within scope, so they may be coverable through the County's section 10 permit, normally by paying the per-acre mitigation fee at grading or building permit. Two points to confirm:
  - (1) Whether the State Lands leasehold at Jean is treated as within the permit area for fee purposes.
  - (2) Whether the MSHCP carve-out for "Federal actions on non-Federal lands" pulls the leasehold facilities out of MSHCP coverage if BLM's ROW decision makes them part of a federal action. If both routes are closed, the developer could face a coverage gap on the leasehold. This is easy to miss.
- Timing: the permit expires on acreage or January 2031. A project that clears after the remaining acreage runs out depends on the amendment.
- The per-acre fee amount was not verified and is not stated here.
