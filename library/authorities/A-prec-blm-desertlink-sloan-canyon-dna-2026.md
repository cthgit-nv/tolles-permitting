## What it is

A ROW for DesertLink, LLC to build a 500 kV loop-in: 12 steel pole structures, about 1 mile of new permanent access road and 0.5 mile of temporary road, connecting the expanded Sloan Canyon Substation to the existing Harry Allen-Eldorado 500 kV line, within a designated utility corridor in Clark County. The disturbance is 5.7 acres permanent and 37.1 acres temporary. BLM relied on the 2012 Eldorado Valley Transmission and Utility Corridor Programmatic EA. Level of review reached: DNA tiered to a programmatic EA.

## Operative text or holding

- Office: Southern Nevada District, Las Vegas Field Office (the worksheet header reads Las Vegas Field Office/Pahrump Field Office).
- Year: DR signed 05-22-2026. An updated DNA and DR were posted 8/13/2026.
- Basis: the corridor programmatic EA analyzed transmission development in the Eldorado Valley corridors, and desert tortoise is covered by the Southern Nevada District Programmatic Biological Opinion.
- What it shows the office accepts: where a programmatic corridor EA exists, LVFO approves new high-voltage line segments and new access roads by DNA. The 2023 Boulder Flats 230 kV DNA (DOI-BLM-NV-S010-2023-0035-DNA) relied on the same 2012 EA.
