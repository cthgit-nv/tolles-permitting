## What it is

The land use plan governing BLM decisions in the Carson City District, which covers Churchill County, is the 2001 Carson City Field Office Consolidated RMP (CRMP), as amended. BLM announced a district-wide revision in February 2012. No revised plan was found, and 2024 and 2025 BLM notices still amend the 2001 CRMP. `status: unverified` because the current status of the 2012 revision (ongoing, paused or terminated) was not confirmed from a primary source. `decided` uses the 2012 NOI date.

## Operative text or holding

- The 2012 NOI announced a revision of the Carson City District RMP and EIS. The planning area is about 5 million acres of public land in portions of 12 counties, including Churchill, Lyon and Washoe.
- The 2001 CRMP is still in use:
  - Greenlink North proposed RMP amendment/Final EIS (May 28, 2025): "would amend the existing 2001 Consolidated Resource Management Plan in Carson City District".
  - Dodge Flat II NOI (Jan. 19, 2024): amends slope direction in "the 2001 Carson City Field Office Consolidated Resource Management Plan".
  - The 2023 Section 368 NOI lists the "Carson City Field Office Consolidated Resource Management Plan, Nevada" among plans to be amended.
- Conformance for transmission: the Greenlink North notice states that the proposed line did not conform to the management objectives of the planning area for transmission lines greater than 100 kV. BLM therefore proposed amending the Carson City, Battle Mountain and Ely plans to designate a utility corridor up to 3,500 feet wide and 198 miles long.

## Notes

- Hazen (Application 2, FMDP): if the FMDP transmission lines exceed 100 kV and the route is not within a designated utility corridor in the CRMP (as amended), the Greenlink North notice indicates BLM may find non-conformance, which would require an RMP amendment. An amendment adds plan-level NEPA, a protest period and Governor's consistency review. A developer who assumes a ROW-only decision could miss this. Two checks are needed:
  - (1) The CRMP's utility corridor and ROW avoidance maps against the FMDP route.
  - (2) Whether the approved Greenlink North corridor, if approved, or the West-wide corridor next to B-16 (see A-usc-frtc-withdrawal) covers the route.
- A 2004 FR notice (04-7001) concerned a CRMP amendment "known as the Churchill County Plan". A 2003 notice (03-18560) concerned a combined CRMP amendment and NAS Fallon plan revision. Neither was read. Both may bear on Churchill County land allocations.
- NDAA 2023 conveyance parcels to Churchill County lie along or near the Greenlink North route. Land status around Hazen may have changed since the 2001 plan.
