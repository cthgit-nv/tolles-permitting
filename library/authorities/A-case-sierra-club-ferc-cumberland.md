## What it is

D.C. Circuit decision (Walker, J.; Katsas, J., joining in full; Pillard, J., joining except Parts II.B.3.a and III) denying review of FERC's certificate and EIS for a 32-mile gas pipeline serving TVA's new gas turbine at the Cumberland Fossil Plant, Tennessee. Consolidated with No. 24-1198.

## Operative text or holding

Part II.B.3.a: after Seven County, the CEQ connected-actions regulation must be read in light of NEPA's text, so an agency is not required to analyze projects over which it has no regulatory authority. FERC has no jurisdiction over electric generation facilities, so the pipeline and TVA's power plant were not connected actions requiring one EIS. Part II.B.3.b (joined by all three judges): even assuming error, it was harmless, because FERC analyzed the plant's effects as cumulative effects, TVA analyzed the pipeline and plant together in its own EIS, and petitioners identified no missing information.

## Quotes

> "In sum, after Seven County, agencies are no longer “required to analyze the effects of projects over which they do not exercise regulatory authority.”" (slip op. at 13)
> "So in NEPA parlance, the proposed pipeline and the TVA’s power plant are not “connected actions” that must be analyzed in the same environmental impact statement." (slip op. at 13)
> "Even if the connected-action requirement did apply, we would not vacate FERC’s approval of the pipeline" (slip op. at 14)
> "After Seven County, the era of searching NEPA review is over" (slip op. at 18)

## Notes

- Majority composition matters. Judge Pillard did not join Part II.B.3.a (the Seven County connected-action holding) or Part III (the conclusion containing the "era of searching NEPA review" line). Those parts are a two-judge majority (Walker and Katsas); the source text contains no separate opinion by Judge Pillard explaining why. Part II.B.3.b (harmless error) is unanimous. When citing for the connected-action point, cite Part II.B.3.a and do not describe it as unanimous.
- The full sentence at slip op. at 18 continues "or at least it should be."; the quote above stops before that phrase.
- The opinion cites Seven County by S. Ct. pages (145 S. Ct. 1497, 1512 to 1516), not U.S. pages.
- Footnote 7 (slip op. at 13) notes the CEQ regulations were amended (89 Fed. Reg. 35442 (May 1, 2024)) and then rescinded (90 Fed. Reg. 10610 (Feb. 25, 2025)), and that the parties agreed the regulations in effect at FERC's decision controlled.
- Directly relevant to projects where a federal permit (pipeline, line, road) serves a facility the permitting agency does not regulate.
