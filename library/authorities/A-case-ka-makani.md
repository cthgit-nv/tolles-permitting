## What it is

Ninth Circuit decision (Tashima, J.) affirming summary judgment that USGS and HUD involvement in a County of Hawaii groundwater transmission project (the Kohala Project) did not make it a major federal action requiring a federal EIS.

## Operative text or holding

Federal funding of about $1.3 million (under 2 percent of an $80 million project) for preliminary studies and EIS preparation did not federalize the local project, applying the funding-disparity factor from Friends of the Earth v. Coleman. USGS's advisory role and HUD's grant advice gave no decision-making power or control; final authority stayed with the county. HUD's connected-actions regulation (24 C.F.R. 58.32) and Thomas v. Peterson did not require an EIS for the whole project before releasing funds for exempt preliminary activities.

## Quotes

> "We therefore conclude that the federal funding contribution alone could not transform the entire Kohala Project into a “major federal action.”" (295 F.3d at 960)
> "Because the final decision-making power remained at all times with DWS, we conclude that the USGS involvement was not sufficient to constitute “major federal action.”" (295 F.3d at 961)
> "In sum, we hold that the actions of HUD and USGS, taken together, in the preliminary stages of the Kohala Project did not constitute “major federal action” within the scope of NEPA." (295 F.3d at 961)
