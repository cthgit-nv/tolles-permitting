## What it is

The ESA section 7 consultation definitions (402.02), including "effects of the action", and the former 402.17 ("Other provisions", the reasonably certain to occur factors), which the Services removed in 2024 and have proposed to reinstate.

## Operative text or holding

Effects of the action are all consequences to listed species or critical habitat caused by the proposed action, including consequences of other activities caused by the action but not part of it. A consequence is caused by the action if it would not occur but for the action and is reasonably certain to occur. Effects may occur later in time and outside the immediate area. 402.17 does not exist in the current CFR: the 2024 final rule (89 FR 24268, effective May 6, 2024) removed it and deleted the cross-reference to it from the 402.02 definition. A November 2025 proposed rule (90 FR 52600) would reinstate 402.17 with an added factor; no final rule found.

## Quotes

> "Effects of the action are all consequences to listed species or critical habitat that are caused by the proposed action, including the consequences of other activities that are caused by the proposed action but that are not part of the action." (50 CFR 402.02)
> "A consequence is caused by the proposed action if it would not occur but for the proposed action and it is reasonably certain to occur." (50 CFR 402.02)
> "As proposed, in this final rule, we are removing Sec. 402.17 in its entirety." (89 FR 24280)
> "However, the Services are now proposing to reinstate Sec. 402.17 to prevent confusion and provide more clarity in the regulatory text." (90 FR 52603)
