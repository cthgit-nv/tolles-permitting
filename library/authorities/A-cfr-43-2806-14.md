## What it is

BLM rent exemption rule for FLPMA rights-of-way.

## Operative text or holding

No rent is owed if (1) the authorizing statute bars rent; (2) the holder is a Federal, state, or local government or its agent or instrumentality, unless it uses the right-of-way for commercial purposes or is a municipal utility or cooperative whose principal source of revenue is customer charges; (3) a statute grants an exemption; or (4) the facilities are electric or telephone facilities financed or eligible for financing under the Rural Electrification Act. Exemptions do not apply to a holder in trespass.

## Quotes

> "You are a Federal, state, or local government or its agent or instrumentality, unless you are:" (43 CFR 2806.14(a)(2))
> "Using the facility, system, space, or any part of the right-of-way area for commercial purposes; or" (43 CFR 2806.14(a)(2)(i))
> "A municipal utility or cooperative whose principal source of revenue is customer charges;" (43 CFR 2806.14(a)(2)(ii))
> "The exemptions in this section do not apply if you are in trespass." (43 CFR 2806.14(b))

## Notes

- A government entity (for example a general improvement district) qualifies under (a)(2) only if neither carve-out applies. Whether a given use is "commercial purposes" is not defined in this section; we did not research IBLA treatment of that phrase in this session.
- A holder that loses the exemption on assignment may see rent increase (43 CFR 2807.21(e), see A-cfr-43-2807-21).
