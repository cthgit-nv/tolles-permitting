## What it is

BLM's own Realty categorical exclusions, now listed in Appendix 2, Section 11.9, paragraph E of the February 2026 DOI NEPA Handbook. The numbers E(1) through E(20) are the same as in former 516 DM 11.9 E, so the memo's E(9), E(12) and E(16) still point to the same text.

## Operative text or holding

- E(9): renewals and assignments of leases, permits or rights-of-way that convey no rights beyond the original grant.
- E(12): grants of right-of-way wholly within the boundaries of other compatibly developed rights-of-way.
- E(13): amendments to existing rights-of-way, such as facility upgrades, with no new disturbance outside the right-of-way boundary.
- E(14): an overhead line crossing a corner of public land, with no pole or tower on BLM land.
- E(16): easements for an existing road, or authorizations to use existing facilities, improvements or sites for the same or similar purposes.
- E(17): short rights-of-way for utility service or terminal access roads to an individual residence, outbuilding or water well.
- E(19): short-term (3 years or less) rights-of-way, such as construction sites, where the land will be rehabilitated.

None of the E list covers a new, stand-alone transmission line, substation or access road on undisturbed land outside an existing developed right-of-way. The closest options are E(12) (only if the new facility sits wholly inside a compatibly developed ROW) and E(14) (corner crossing with no structure on BLM land). The adopted CEs that do reach new transmission lines and substations are in A-guid-blm-adopted-utility-cx. The E list carries no asterisk, but the 11.9 introduction says CEs adopted under Section 109 or marked with an asterisk must be documented, and BLM recommends documenting the others.

> "The following BLM actions are designated categorical exclusions unless one or more of the Department’s extraordinary circumstances apply." (App. 2, 11.9, handbook p. 64)

## Treatment

## Notes

- Memo check: E(9), E(12) and E(16) are correctly numbered under the current Handbook. The memo should cite "DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, 11.9(E)(12)" and so on, not 516 DM 11.9.
- For FMDP, E(12) could fit segments placed wholly inside the existing NV Energy line and road ROW, if BLM agrees the existing ROW is "compatibly developed". Segments on new ground need another CE or an EA.
- 43 C.F.R. 46.205(f) (combining several CEs for a composite action) and 46.205(g) (any bureau may use any Department or bureau CE) are carded in A-cfr-43-46.
