## What it is

The FY2023 NDAA title that modernized and expanded the Navy's Fallon Range Training Complex (FRTC) land withdrawal in Churchill, Lyon, Mineral, Pershing and Nye Counties. It amends the Military Land Withdrawals Act of 2013 (Pub. L. 113-66). The same title also designates wilderness and national conservation areas, provides for conveyances to Churchill County (sec. 2907), and deals with tribal lands and access. `decided` is the enactment date of Pub. L. 117-263 (Dec. 23, 2022), which was not confirmed from the enrolled text in this session.

## Operative text or holding

- Sec. 2981: withdraws about 790,825 acres. The B-16, B-17, B-19 and B-20 ranges are closed to public land entry, mining location, and mineral and geothermal leasing. The Dixie Valley Training Area is closed to entry and mining location only.
- Sec. 2982(b)(5): in the Dixie Valley Training Area, livestock grazing and geothermal exploration and development west of State Route 121 remain permitted, managed by BLM in coordination with the Navy. Sec. 2982(b)(6) requires water and utility infrastructure to be allowed there.
- Sec. 2991(c): existing roads and rights-of-way on US 50 and 95, SR 121 and 839, and named county roads are unaffected.
- Sec. 2991(e): the "Churchill County Preferred I-11 Corridor" and "NDOT I-11 Corridor" are to be managed under the section.
- Sec. 2991(g) and (h): land in the mapped "Utility and Infrastructure Corridors" is withdrawn from mining and from mineral and geothermal leasing until Interior, in coordination with Churchill County, terminates the withdrawal, or the land is patented.
- Sec. 2991(j), on the West-wide Energy Corridor:
  - Nothing in the withdrawal restricts high-voltage lines in the part of the designated West-Wide Energy Corridor that lies outside the B-16 Range.
  - The Navy must allow one transmission line inside the B-16 Range, in the part of the corridor nearest the existing transmission line along B-16's western boundary.
  - If Interior and the Navy decide more lines cannot fit outside B-16, the Navy must allow a new line as close as practicable to the existing line.
- Sec. 2999A: the withdrawal ends November 6, 2047.

## Quotes

> "the land established as the B-16, B-17, B-19, and B-20 Ranges, as referred to in subsection (b), and all other areas within the boundary of such land as depicted on the map entitled `Churchill County Proposed Fallon Range Training Complex Modernization and Lands Bill' and dated November 30, 2022" (sec. 2981(a)(1))
> "Nothing in section 2981 shall be construed to restrict the development of high voltage electrical power utility lines" (sec. 2991(j)(1))
> "The Secretary of the Navy shall allow 1 transmission line within that portion of the designated West-Wide Energy Corridor that is located within the B-16 Range nearest the existing transmission line adjacent to the western boundary of the B-16 Range." (sec. 2991(j)(2))
> "The withdrawal and reservation of public land by section 2981 shall terminate on November 6, 2047." (sec. 2999A)

## Notes

- Hazen (Application 2, FMDP): sec. 2991(j) is the first primary-source confirmation found of a designated West-wide (Section 368) energy corridor next to the B-16 Range, with an existing transmission line along B-16's western boundary. Two points were not verified: (1) where B-16 and that corridor lie relative to the Hazen site and the FMDP route, and (2) the corridor number.
  - The existing line may be the NV Energy line the FMDP corridor shares; that is not verified.
  - If FMDP lines route through B-16, the statute allows only one additional line inside the range, so capacity for later lines there is limited.
  - Get the November 30, 2022 map. BLM must keep it on file under sec. 2991(f).
- Geothermal: the mineral and geothermal leasing withdrawal covers the B ranges and the Utility and Infrastructure Corridors. Federal geothermal leasing for a Hazen geothermal plant would be foreclosed on those lands but not on the Dixie Valley Training Area west of SR 121. Private-land geothermal is unaffected by the withdrawal.
- Sec. 2907 (conveyances to Churchill County) and sec. 2989 (trust land for the Fallon Paiute-Shoshone Tribe) were not read in full. Read both before any Hazen land status statement.
