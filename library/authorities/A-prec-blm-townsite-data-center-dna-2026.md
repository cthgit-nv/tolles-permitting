## What it is

The Las Vegas Field Office amended an existing solar ROW grant (N-99875, Townsite Solar 2 LLC) to allow a data center on 81 acres of the 85-acre ROW area, plus a 4-acre, 80 by 2,000 foot linear facilities strip carrying a site road, an overhead 12 to 13.8 kV distribution line, an underground 34.5 to 230 kV collection line, and fiber. The data center also includes a 70 MW BESS, an electrical substation, and 167 MW of biodiesel backup generators. BLM relied on the 2023 EA for the solar project through a DNA. Level of review reached: DNA (no new EA).

## Operative text or holding

- Office: Southern Nevada District, Las Vegas Field Office. Location: northern Eldorado Valley, Clark County (T. 23 S., R. 63 E., Sec. 14).
- Year: Register start 5/11/2026, decision 6/26/2026.
- Rationale: the new use sits within the footprint analyzed in the 2023 EA, the effects are similar, the EA is less than three years old with no new circumstances, and denial would not leave the land undeveloped because the solar project is already authorized.
- What it shows the office accepts: LVFO will change the use of an existing energy ROW to a data center and add linear power and fiber facilities without a new EA, where a recent EA covered the same footprint.

## Quotes

> "The Applicant now proposes to construct, own, operate, maintain, and eventually decommission a data center on 81 acres of the existing 85-acre ROW area, with an additional 4 acres to be used for an 80 × 2,000–foot linear facilities segment" (DNA p. 1)
> "Installation of 167 MW of back-up biodiesel-fired generators." (DNA p. 3)
> "In other words, a denial of the new proposed action does not mean that the project area in question would remain undeveloped." (DNA, criterion 3)
> "Because the 2023 Environmental Assessment is still less than three years old and there are no known new circumstances or information regarding the condition of resources present at the project location" (DNA, criterion 4)
