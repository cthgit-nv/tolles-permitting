## What it is

A recent Board decision on standing to appeal under 43 C.F.R. Part 4. The Board dismissed a county's appeals of four BLM travel management plans because the county did not show that the decisions were substantially likely to injure a legally cognizable interest. The source file also holds the current Part 4, Subpart E standing text.

## Operative text or holding

- An appellant must be both a "party to the case" and "adversely affected", and it bears the burden on both. If either is missing, the Board must dismiss.
- Adversely affected means a legally cognizable interest that the decision has injured or is substantially likely to injure. Allegations must be colorable, specific, and causally linked. Speculative or vague harms fail.
- A procedural injury alone, such as an alleged NEPA violation, does not give standing unless it is tied to injury to a separate cognizable interest.
- Current rule: IBLA's procedural rules were rewritten by an interim final rule effective February 10, 2025 (90 FR 2332). Standing is now at 43 C.F.R. 4.402(a), with "adversely affected" defined in 4.401. The notice of appeal must include a statement of facts showing standing (4.403(a)(2)). This decision cites the older numbering (4.410(a), (b), (d)), which applied when the 2024 appeals were filed (inferred, not confirmed).

## Quotes

> "An appellant bears the burden to demonstrate that it satisfies both elements," (200 IBLA at 59)
> "But procedural injuries, alone, cannot provide an independent basis for standing." (200 IBLA at 64)
> "Any person or entity that is a party to the case and is adversely affected by an appealable decision of a bureau or office or an ALJ has the right to appeal to the Board, except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section." (43 C.F.R. 4.402(a))
> "Effective date: These rule is effective February 10, 2025." (90 FR 2332, DATES; "These rule" is the original wording)

## Notes

- Memo check: any memo that cites 43 C.F.R. 4.410 for standing is out of date. 4.410 is now "Briefs". Standing is 4.402, the definitions are 4.401, how to appeal is 4.403, and stays are 4.405. The 30-day deadline to appeal runs from receipt of notice of the decision (4.403(c)).
- 4.410(b)(3) now limits issues on appeal to those raised to the bureau by any participant, or issues that arose after the participation opportunity closed. That matters for issue preservation in EA comment periods.
- Also useful: A-ibla-santa-fe-nw-info-council (users of an access road had standing against a ROW) and A-ibla-cbd-lincoln-county-row (organizational standing test).
- 4.418 (new) makes Board decisions precedential and dispositive orders non-precedential.
