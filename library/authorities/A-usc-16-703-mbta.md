## What it is

The MBTA makes it unlawful to take or kill any protected migratory bird, nest or egg "by any means or in any manner", except as permitted by regulation. `decided` is the 1918 enactment date, which was not verified from the session laws in this session. There is no incidental take permit program for transmission lines.

## Operative text or holding

- 703(a) prohibits take "at any time, by any means or in any manner" unless regulations permit it.
- Interior's current position: Solicitor's Opinion M-37085 (Apr. 11, 2025) withdrew M-37065 (2021). It directs all bureaus to treat M-37050 (Dec. 2017) as authoritative and binding. M-37050 concluded the MBTA does not apply to incidental take. The exception is actions within the jurisdiction of the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of New York, which vacated M-37050 in 2020.
- On April 21, 2025, FWS withdrew its October 2021 advance notice of proposed rulemaking on authorizing incidental take (90 FR 16664). No general incidental take permit rule exists.

## Quotes

> "it shall be unlawful at any time, by any means or in any manner, to pursue, hunt, take, capture, kill, attempt to take, capture, or kill, possess" (16 U.S.C. 703(a))
> "M-37065 is repealed." (M-37085, at 1)
> "within the jurisdiction of the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York." (M-37085, at 1)
> "The advance notice of proposed rulemaking that published on October 4, 2021, at 86 FR 54667, is withdrawn as of April 21, 2025." (90 FR 16664)

## Notes
