## What it is

BLM right-of-way cost recovery rule. Sets six processing and monitoring categories by estimated Federal work hours.

## Operative text or holding

Applicants pay cost recovery fees for reasonable costs (as defined in FLPMA 304(b)) unless waived under 2804.16. Categories 1 to 4 are one-time, non-refundable fees tied to work hours (up to 8, 8 to 24, 24 to 40, 40 to 64). Category 5 is a Master Agreement. Category 6 applies when work exceeds 64 hours or an EIS is required. BLM notifies the applicant of the category after initial review; processing starts after payment. BLM may move an application to Category 6 at any time if an EIS is needed. Category decisions are appealable under 2801.10. Under (f), an applicant may waive the reasonable-cost factors and elect to pay actual costs to expedite processing.

## Quotes

> "Category 6 fees are for situations when a right-of-way activity will require more than 64 hours, or when an environmental impact statement (EIS) is required and may include preliminary application review costs." (43 CFR 2804.14(b))
> "You must then submit to the BLM the appropriate payment for that category before the BLM will begin processing your application." (43 CFR 2804.14(d))
> "In processing your application, the BLM may determine at any time that the application requires preparing an EIS." (43 CFR 2804.14(e))
> "are electing to pay the actual costs incurred by the BLM in processing your application and monitoring your grant" (43 CFR 2804.14(f))
