## What it is

NEPA section 112, codified at 42 U.S.C. 4336f, added by Pub. L. 119-21 (the 2025 reconciliation act), title VI, section 60026. A project sponsor may pay a fee in exchange for shortened EA and EIS deadlines.

## Operative text or holding

- (a)(1): the sponsor submits to CEQ a project description and a declaration of whether it intends to prepare the document itself under 4336a(f).
- (a)(2): within 15 days CEQ notifies the sponsor of the fee amount.
- (a)(3): the sponsor may pay after that notice.
- (a)(4): notwithstanding 4336a(g)(1), an EA for which the fee is paid must be completed within 180 days after the fee is paid; an EIS within 1 year after publication of the notice of intent.
- (b): the fee is 125 percent of the anticipated costs to prepare the document, or, for a sponsor-prepared document, 125 percent of the anticipated costs to supervise preparation of (and, as applicable, prepare) it.

## Quotes

> "an environmental assessment for which a fee is paid under this section shall be completed not later than 180 days after the date on which the fee is paid" (42 U.S.C. 4336f(a)(4)(A))
> "an environmental impact statement for which a fee is paid under this section shall be completed not later than 1 year after the date of publication of the notice of intent to prepare the environmental impact statement" (42 U.S.C. 4336f(a)(4)(B))
> "125 percent of the anticipated costs to prepare the environmental assessment or environmental impact statement" (42 U.S.C. 4336f(b)(1))
> "the Council shall provide to the project sponsor notice of the amount of the fee to be paid under this section" (42 U.S.C. 4336f(a)(2))

## Notes

- Confirmed: 125 percent fee, 180-day EA deadline, 1-year EIS deadline.
- The two clocks start differently: the EA clock runs from fee payment; the EIS clock runs from publication of the notice of intent, not from payment.
- The fee is set by CEQ, not by the lead agency. The section as codified does not say how the fee is split or refunded, what happens if the agency misses the shortened deadline, or whether the 4336a(g)(3) court petition applies to the shortened deadline. We did not find CEQ implementing guidance on the fee in this session.
