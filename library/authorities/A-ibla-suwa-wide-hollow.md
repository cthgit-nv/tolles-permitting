## What it is

An adverse decision. The Board set aside and remanded a BLM (Grand Staircase-Escalante National Monument) decision record and FONSI, and the ROW grant based on them, for a new reservoir with diversion structures, pipelines and roads. The FONSI relied on mitigation that BLM had not shown would reduce riparian impacts below significance.

## Operative text or holding

A FONSI stands if BLM took a hard look, identified the relevant concerns, and made a convincing case that impacts are insignificant or will be made insignificant by mitigation. Where the FONSI depends on mitigation, the EA must analyze how effective that mitigation will be. A monitoring-and-adjust plan that reacts only after harm appears did not make a convincing case. The Board also dismissed one related appeal for lack of standing because the appellant was not a "party to the case" (footnote 1).

## Quotes

> "A mitigation plan must be sufficiently developed and explained to provide a convincing case that significant environmental impacts will be reduced to insignificance." (166 IBLA at 141, headnote 1)
> "A FONSI will be set aside where an appellant has shown that the proposed actions will have a significant impact to riparian resources and that BLM has failed to demonstrate that the proposed mitigation measures will reduce those impacts to insignificance." (166 IBLA at 141, headnote 1)
> "Applying these principles to the appeals before us, we find that BLM did not take the requisite hard look at the project’s potentially significant impacts to riparian areas and failed to convincingly demonstrate that the incorporated mitigation measures would reduce those impacts to insignificance." (166 IBLA at 174)

## Notes

- Relevance: this is the main risk for a mitigated FONSI on a linear ROW. If the Hazen or Jean EA depends on applicant-committed measures to avoid significance (for example desert tortoise measures at Jean), the EA must explain how well they work, not just list them.
- The standard it applies ("convincing case") comes from pre-2025 IBLA practice under CEQ rules. The DOI NEPA Handbook's FONSI guidance (App. 1) now governs the form of the FONSI. This decision still states the Board's review standard.
- The reservoir facts (dam, flooding a riparian area) are more severe than a utility ROW, so it can be distinguished on the facts.
