## What it is

The Handbook's list of CEs that BLM (and some other DOI bureaus) adopted from DOE, TVA, RUS, USACE and NTIA under NEPA section 109, 42 U.S.C. 4336c. The utility entries in 16.1(E) reach new transmission lines, substations, short pipelines and fiber, which BLM's own Realty list at 11.9(E) does not.

## Operative text or holding

The utility-related adopted CEs most relevant to Hazen and Jean. All carry an asterisk, so they must be documented and screened for extraordinary circumstances.

- 16.1(E)(20), TVA n.16 (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 42432): new transmission lines generally no more than 10 miles long, with no more than 125 acres of new developed ROW and no more than 1 mile of new access road outside the ROW, and/or substations generally disturbing no more than 10 acres.
- 16.1(E)(22), RUS 7 CFR 1970.54(c)(2) (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 42432): new power lines under 69 kV; under 230 kV if no more than 25 miles; or 230 kV and up if no more than 3 miles and not for integrating major new generation.
- 16.1(E)(17), DOE B4.11 (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 42432): construction or modification of substations and interconnection facilities.
- 16.1(E)(16), USACE 33 CFR 230.9(i) (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 42432): ROW grants with only minor disturbance for minor access roads and minor utility distribution lines.
- 16.1(E)(13), TVA n.17 (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 27655): upgrades to existing transmission, plus new access roads outside the ROW generally no more than 1 mile long.
- 16.1(E)(3), DOE B4.7, and 16.1(E)(12), NTIA C-8 (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 4774): fiber on transmission facilities or buried in existing powerline or pipeline ROWs, and utilities or communication lines using existing ROWs.
- 16.1(E)(18), DOE B5.5 (adopted by BLM, 90 FR 42432): pipeline segments generally under 20 miles, within previously disturbed or developed ROWs.

The introduction says any bureau or program may apply any appropriate adopted CE.

(App. 2, 16.1(E)(20), handbook p. 109)

## Notes

- DOE CEs must be applied with DOE's "Integral Elements" (10 CFR 1021 Appendix B). The Handbook says this for each DOE entry. Check them before relying on B4.11 or B4.7.
- For Jean (substation, transmission lines, access road, fiber) and FMDP (transmission lines and access roads), TVA n.16 combined with DOE B4.11 or NTIA C-8 may cover a composite action. 43 C.F.R. 46.205(f) (see A-cfr-43-46) allows combining CEs if each element is covered and the extraordinary circumstances review is done for the whole. Whether the size limits are met is a project-fact question.
- No BLM Nevada decision applying TVA n.16 or RUS 1970.54(c)(2) was found in this session's NEPA Register searches. See the research log.
