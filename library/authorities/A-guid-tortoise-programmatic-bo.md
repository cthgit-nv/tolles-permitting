## What it is

This card has two parts.

1. The listing status of the desert tortoise (Mojave population), taken from 50 CFR 17.11(h).
2. The existence and file number of the FWS programmatic biological opinion (PBO) for the BLM Southern Nevada District Office (SNDO), taken from FWS's ECOS catalog of issued biological opinions.

The PBO itself was not retrieved, so its terms are unverified. That is the reason `status: unverified`.

## Operative text or holding

- Listing: the desert tortoise (Mojave DPS), *Gopherus agassizii*, is listed as threatened wherever found, except Arizona south and east of the Colorado River and Mexico. The listing citations are 45 FR 55654 (8/20/1980), 54 FR 32326 (8/4/1989) and 55 FR 12178 (4/2/1990). Critical habitat is at 50 CFR 17.95(c).
- PBO: ECOS lists "BLM SNDO Reinitiation of the 1998 RMP PBO", File No. 08ENVS00-2019-F-0153, dated 01/14/2020. ECOS also lists many later project-specific consultations titled "Append ... to SNDO PBO", including ROW actions, so individual SNDO projects are appended to the PBO rather than consulted on separately. Examples:
  - Sloan to Mead Rebuild Transmission Line ROW (08ENVS00-2020-F-0028, 01/14/2020);
  - Via Nobila to I15 ROW (08ENVS00-2020-F-0014);
  - Miners Mesa Road ROW (08ENVS00-2020-F-0100);
  - Southern Nevada Interie Project - Desertlink (08ENVD00-2015-F-0034);
  - Faraday Future Apex Gas Line Project (08ENVS00-2017-F-0061).
- Large solar projects such as Gemini and Yellow Pine appear as separate consultations, not appends.
- Northern Ivanpah Valley: in July 2024 BLM's Las Vegas Field Office proposed a habitat connectivity and restoration project in and around the Large-Scale Translocation Site. This is a fenced area of about 40 square miles into which FWS released over 9,000 tortoises from 1997 to 2014.
