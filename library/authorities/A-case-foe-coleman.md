## What it is

Ninth Circuit decision (Eugene A. Wright, J.) affirming the partial denial of a preliminary injunction in a challenge to San Francisco International Airport's expansion program. No. 74-3490.

## Operative text or holding

Locally funded airport projects (a new north terminal and a parking garage) were not federal actions merely because other projects in the same master plan received FAA airport development aid. They were distinct projects with separate functions and independent justifications, local officials were committed to them with or without federal money, and federal funds were less than 10 percent of total program spending. Whether federal and nonfederal projects form a single federal action turns on all the facts; at some point the nexus becomes close enough to require joint review.

## Quotes

> "We also conclude that the state-funded projects are not so closely interwoven with those receiving federal funds to make the entire airport development program the relevant “action” for NEPA purposes." (518 F.2d at 327)
> "In the case before us, there was no attempt to fragment a project for which federal aid had been an open option for the purpose of avoiding compliance with NEPA." (518 F.2d at 328)
> "NEPA aims to bring environmental considerations to the attention of federal decisionmakers but, except on the ADAP-funded projects, the only relevant decision makers here are local officials." (518 F.2d at 329)
> "At some point, the nexus will become so close, and the projects so intertwined, that they will require joint NEPA evaluation." (518 F.2d at 329)
