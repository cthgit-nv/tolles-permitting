## What it is

The Board affirmed a BLM Burns District decision granting a short-term ROW for two meteorological towers (wind testing) under Realty CX E(19), with no EA or EIS. The appellant argued the ROW could be renewed beyond 3 years and so fell outside the CX.

## Operative text or holding

- Standard of review: BLM's decision to rely on a CX is affirmed if it is reasonable and supported by the record. BLM's reading of its own CX must not be plainly erroneous.
- Two steps: (1) does the action fit a CX category, and (2) do any extraordinary circumstances apply.
- A possible future renewal did not take the grant outside E(19), because the grant made any renewal subject to later policy and a separate application with its own NEPA review. The grant disclaimed any authority for commercial development.
- The appellant did not rebut BLM's finding of no extraordinary circumstances.

## Quotes

> "When BLM decides not to prepare an EA or EIS, because the proposed action qualifies for a categorical exclusion, we have long held that the decision will be affirmed on review where it is reasonable and supported by the record." (174 IBLA at 33)
> "Finally, BLM determined that there were no extraordinary circumstances that might militate against application of categorical exclusion E(19).  ONDA has not demonstrated otherwise." (174 IBLA at 34)

## Notes

- The CX numbering then was 516 DM 11.5 E(19), which is now DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, 11.9(E)(19) (same text). See A-guid-blm-realty-cx.
- Earlier, BLM had issued the same grant for more than 3 years, the Board remanded it, and BLM reissued it within the 3-year limit. A CX must fit its terms exactly.
- Relevance: this is the standard of review for a CX-based ROW decision. The appellant carries the burden on extraordinary circumstances. Keeping future phases out of the grant, with separate NEPA required for them, helped BLM's CX hold up. That approach may help keep a CX for a narrow first-phase grant, subject to the segmentation limits in A-ibla-defenders-wildlife-ajo.
- No IBLA decision found in this session reviews a BLM CX for a new transmission line or substation ROW. See the research log.
