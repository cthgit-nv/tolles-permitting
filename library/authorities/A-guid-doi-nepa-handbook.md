## What it is

The Department of the Interior's NEPA procedures document, issued as 516 DM 1 and dated February 2026 (the landing page shows February 23, 2026). It works alongside the trimmed 43 C.F.R. Part 46 (final rule at 91 FR 8738, Feb. 24, 2026) and replaces the former bureau chapters of 516 DM (such as 516 DM 11 for BLM and 516 DM 14 for Reclamation) and the rescinded parts of Part 46. Appendix 1 is implementation guidance. Appendix 2 lists every bureau CE and the CEs adopted from other agencies.

## Operative text or holding

- The Handbook and 43 C.F.R. Part 46 together set out DOI's NEPA procedures. The Handbook says it creates no enforceable rights and does not itself have the force of law.
- NEPA does not apply to non-Federal actions. Control over only a small part of an action is generally not enough to make the action Federal (sec. 1.1).
- A connected action is defined as a separate Federal action (sec. 6.1(g)). The definition does not reach private actions.
- CE review: a bureau may use CEs in 43 C.F.R. 46.210 or Appendix 2, may use other bureaus' CEs, may combine CEs, and must screen for extraordinary circumstances under 46.215 before relying on a CE. The screen covers the proposed action considered together with any connected actions (sec. 1.4 and Appendix 1).
- Citation style: "DOI NEPA Handbook § 2.3(a)(3)" or "DOI NEPA Handbook Appendix § 2". Appendix 2 CEs are cited "DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, [section], [paragraph]".

## Quotes

> "Citations to this Handbook should be styled in the following format, using these specific citations as examples: DOI NEPA Handbook § 2.3(a)(3) or DOI NEPA Handbook Appendix § 2." (Handbook, unnumbered page after the cover)
> "This document, along with 43 CFR Part 46, sets forth the Department of the Interior (DOI or Department) procedures for implementing NEPA." (sec. 0.1(a), p. 1)
> "Although the Handbook may in places restate statutory or regulatory requirements, the procedures in this Handbook themselves do not have the force or effect of law." (sec. 0.1 n.1, p. 1)
> "Control over only a small part of an action is generally not sufficient to make an action “Federal” such that a NEPA obligation attaches." (sec. 1.1, p. 2)
> "Connected action means a separate Federal action within the authority of a Federal agency that is closely related to the proposed action and should be addressed in a single environmental document" (sec. 6.1(g), p. 23)
> "If the proposed action, considered together with any connected actions, would implicate any of the extraordinary circumstances, and the bureau cannot eliminate those extraordinary circumstances by modifying the proposed action to reduce or remove potentially significant impacts, then the bureau would need to develop an environmental assessment or environmental impact statement." (App. 1, CE protocol, p. 32)
> "A bureau must evaluate the proposed action for the presence of any of the extraordinary circumstances listed at 43 CFR 46.215 before that bureau relies on a categorical exclusion." (sec. 1.4(e), p. 7)
