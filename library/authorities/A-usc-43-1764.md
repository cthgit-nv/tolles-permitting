## What it is

FLPMA section 504, general requirements for rights-of-way. Subsection (g) sets the rent obligation, the authority to charge less than fair market value, and the authority to recover processing and monitoring costs.

## Operative text or holding

The holder pays fair market value rent in advance. The Secretary may waive rent for rights-of-way conveyed in a cooperative cost share program. The Secretary may require reimbursement of reasonable administrative and other costs for processing and for inspection and monitoring. Rights-of-way may be granted at less than fair market value (including free use) to Federal, State, or local governments or their agencies or instrumentalities, to certain nonprofits, and to holders providing a valuable public benefit, where the Secretary finds it equitable and in the public interest; such below-market grants are not assignable without approval. Rights-of-way for electric or telephone facilities eligible for Rural Electrification Act financing are granted without rental fees.

## Quotes

> "The holder of a right-of-way shall pay in advance the fair market value thereof, as determined by the Secretary granting, issuing, or renewing such right-of-way." (43 U.S.C. 1764(g))
> "Rights-of-way may be granted, issued, or renewed to a Federal, State, or local government or any agency or instrumentality thereof" (43 U.S.C. 1764(g))
> "Such rights-of-way issued at less than fair market value are not assignable except with the approval of the Secretary issuing the right-of-way." (43 U.S.C. 1764(g))
> "Rights-of-way shall be granted, issued, or renewed, without rental fees, for electric or telephone facilities eligible for financing pursuant to the Rural Electrification Act of 1936" (43 U.S.C. 1764(g))

## Notes

- The statute makes government rent relief discretionary ("may", on an equitable and public interest finding). The regulation, 43 CFR 2806.14(a)(2), converts it into an exemption with a commercial-use carve-out (see A-cfr-43-2806-14).
