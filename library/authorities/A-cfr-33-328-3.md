## What it is

The Corps' codified definition of "waters of the United States" (parallel to EPA's 40 CFR 120.2). The agencies call it the "Amended 2023 Rule": the January 2023 rule as amended by the September 2023 conforming rule after Sackett v. EPA.

## Operative text or holding

Jurisdictional waters: (a)(1) traditional navigable waters, territorial seas, interstate waters; (2) impoundments; (3) relatively permanent tributaries; (4) wetlands adjacent to (a)(1) waters, or to relatively permanent (a)(2)/(a)(3) waters with a continuous surface connection; (5) relatively permanent intrastate lakes and ponds with a continuous surface connection. (b) lists eight exclusions, including dry-land ditches without relatively permanent flow and swales and erosional features. (c)(2) defines adjacent as having a continuous surface connection. The significant nexus standard no longer appears.

## Quotes

> "Tributaries of waters identified in paragraph (a)(1) or (2) of this section that are relatively permanent, standing or continuously flowing bodies of water;" (33 CFR 328.3(a)(3))
> "Adjacent means having a continuous surface connection." (33 CFR 328.3(c)(2))
> "Swales and erosional features (e.g., gullies, small washes) characterized by low volume, infrequent, or short duration flow." (33 CFR 328.3(b)(8))
> "The EPA and the Army are implementing the Amended 2023 Rule in 24 States, the District of Columbia, and the U.S. Territories." (90 FR 52512)
