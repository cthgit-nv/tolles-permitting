## What it is

Interior's codified NEPA procedures. After the July 2025 interim final rule and the February 2026 final rule, Part 46 contains only eight sections; the rest of Interior's procedures are in the DOI NEPA Handbook.

## Operative text or holding

Current Part 46 (eCFR, 2026-09-22) contains: 46.105 (bureau-directed contractors), 46.107 (applicant-prepared EAs and EISs), 46.150 (emergencies), 46.205 (categorical exclusions), 46.210 (Departmental categorical exclusions), 46.215 (extraordinary circumstances), 46.220 (lead agencies), 46.225 (cooperating agencies). Subparts A, D, and E are reserved.

- 46.105: a Responsible Official may use a bureau-directed contractor for any environmental document, remains responsible for it, and must require a professional integrity statement.
- 46.107: implements 42 U.S.C. 4336a(f). The Responsible Official has discretion to allow an applicant or applicant-directed contractor to prepare an EA or EIS; applicants may not prepare decision documents; the bureau independently evaluates and approves; written expectations within 30 days of initiating preparation; an applicant-side professional integrity statement is required.
- 46.210: lists Departmental categorical exclusions (a) through (l); only (k) and (l) require documentation; (k) (hazardous fuels) is not for use within the Ninth Circuit.
- 46.220: lead and joint lead designation.
- 46.225: eligible cooperating agencies; the lead bureau may invite for an EA and must invite for an EIS; requests may not be arbitrarily denied.

## Quotes

> "A Responsible Official may use a bureau-directed contractor to prepare any environmental document." (43 CFR 46.105(a))
> "Applicants or applicant-directed contractors may not prepare decision documents, including records of decision." (43 CFR 46.107(b))
> "Such engagement must occur within 30 days of the date initiating the preparation of an environmental impact statement or an environmental assessment." (43 CFR 46.107(d))
> "must invite eligible agencies to participate as cooperating agencies when the bureau is developing an environmental impact statement" (43 CFR 46.225(b))
