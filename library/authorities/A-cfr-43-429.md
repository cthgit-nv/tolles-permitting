## What it is

Reclamation's rule requiring written authorization for any possession, occupancy, or resource extraction on Reclamation land, facilities, or waterbodies, and setting the application, fee, terms, and appeal process for "use authorizations".

## Operative text or holding

- 429.1 and 429.3: possession or occupancy of Reclamation land, facilities, or waterbodies requires a use authorization; covered uses include infrastructure such as transportation, telecommunications, utilities, and pipelines.
- 429.2: a use authorization is the document setting terms for use; it may take the form of an easement, lease, license, permit, or consent document. A consent document is a written agreement or notification listing conditions that prevent unreasonable interference with a Reclamation easement on non-Reclamation land. Reclamation land includes interests such as easements and 1890 Canal Act rights.
- 429.4: exclusions, including relocation or replacement of facilities under section 14 of the Reclamation Project Act of 1939 and activities authorized under other Federal statutes.
- 429.6: easements and rights-of-way over 25 years also need approval of repayment-obligated water user organizations.
- 429.7: where Reclamation holds only an easement, the applicant seeks a consent document and also needs the landowner's permission.
- 429.10: SF 299 is the form for energy, transportation, water, or telecommunication systems.
- 429.30: no transfer or assignment without prior written approval.

## Quotes

> "Use authorization means a document that defines the terms and conditions under which we will allow you to use Reclamation land, facilities, and waterbodies." (43 CFR 429.2)
> "Use authorizations can take the form of easements, leases, licenses, permits, and consent documents." (43 CFR 429.2)
> "Consent document means a written agreement or notification listing conditions which will prevent unreasonable interference with our easement on non-Reclamation land." (43 CFR 429.2)
> "Use authorizations for easements and rights-of-way for periods in excess of 25 years are also subject to approval from water user organizations under contract obligation for repayment of the project or division." (43 CFR 429.6(a))
