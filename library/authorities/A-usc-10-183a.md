## What it is

The statute establishing the DoD clearinghouse (commonly called the DoD Siting Clearinghouse) that reviews energy projects and certain antenna structures for adverse impact on military operations and readiness. Added by Pub. L. 115-91 (2017), last amended by Pub. L. 119-60 (Dec. 18, 2025).

## Operative text or holding

- (b): the Clearinghouse coordinates DoD review of energy project applications filed with the Secretary of Transportation under 49 U.S.C. 44718 (FAA obstruction evaluation).
- (c)(1): within 75 days of receiving a proper application from DOT, it conducts a preliminary review assessing scope, duration, and risk of adverse impact and identifying feasible and affordable mitigation.
- (c)(2)-(3): if adverse impact is found, it issues a notice of presumed risk to the applicant and the governor; the notice is preliminary, not a formal objection.
- (e): the Secretary may object only on a finding of unacceptable risk to national security, after full consideration of mitigation; delegation is limited.
- (f): DoD may accept voluntary contributions from applicants to offset mitigation costs.
- (h)(4): "energy project" means a project providing for generation or transmission of electrical energy.

## Quotes

> "The Clearinghouse shall coordinate Department of Defense review of applications for energy projects or antenna structure projects filed with the Secretary of Transportation pursuant to section 44718 of title 49" (10 U.S.C. 183a(b)(1))
> "Not later than 75 days after receiving from the Secretary of Transportation a proper application for an energy project or antenna structure project under section 44718 of title 49" (10 U.S.C. 183a(c)(1))
> "A notice of presumed risk issued under subparagraph (A) is a preliminary assessment only and does not represent a formal objection pursuant to subsection (e)." (10 U.S.C. 183a(c)(2)(C))

## Notes

- The section heading is now "Military Aviation and Installation Assurance Clearinghouse for review of mission obstructions"; (a)(1) names it the "Military Aviation and Installation Assurance Siting Clearinghouse". Documents calling it the "DoD Siting Clearinghouse" are using the informal name.
- The statutory trigger is a filing with DOT/FAA under 49 U.S.C. 44718, not a NEPA document or a BLM application. The section does not use the word "range"; it speaks of "military operations and readiness", which (h)(1) defines to include training and testing. (c)(6) and (c)(7) also provide for review requests and early outreach from other agencies, States, landowners, and developers.
- We did not check which Pub. L. 119-60 change was made at sec. 1701(a)(5); it may be a conforming or technical amendment. Unverified.
