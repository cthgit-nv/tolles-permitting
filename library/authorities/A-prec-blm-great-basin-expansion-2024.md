## What it is

BLM's decision to amend Great Basin Gas Transmission Company's existing pipeline ROW (NVN-0060169) and grant temporary use areas for about 3.4 miles of upsized or looped pipeline in Douglas, Lyon and Storey Counties. BLM served as a cooperating agency on FERC's EA and adopted it. Level of review reached: EA (FERC-prepared, adopted by BLM), with FONSI and Decision Record.

## Operative text or holding

- Office: BLM Sierra Front Field Office is the lead office on the NEPA Register. The DR letterhead reads "Carson City District, Stillwater Field Office".
- Year: DR dated July 2024. The Register shows decision date 7/5/2024.
- Record: BLM relied on the FERC EA and FERC FONSI and did not prepare its own EA. The DR states the decision is effective on issuance under 43 C.F.R. 2801.10 and is appealable to IBLA.
- What it shows the office accepts: for a FERC-jurisdictional Great Basin pipeline, the Carson City District has used FERC's NEPA document for its ROW decision instead of a separate BLM analysis.

## Quotes

> "Great Basin proposed to install a 20” natural gas pipeline withing the existing authorization" (Decision Record p. 1; "withing" is the original spelling)

## Notes

- Register data: NEPA No. DOI-BLM-NV-C020-2024-0028-EA; applicant Great Basin Transmission Company; case file NVN-0060169-04; program Lands and Realty, Rights-of-Way; start 2/1/2024; decision 7/5/2024; "FONSI Date 7/8/2023" (probably a data-entry error, since the FERC EA file name carries the date 20231128).
- The FERC EA (20231128-3020, CP23-466-000) is listed on the Register Documents page but was not downloaded.
- FMDP link: Great Basin's 2028 Expansion Project (FERC Docket PF26-5-000; scoping notice 91 FR 23262, Apr. 30, 2026) would add about 195 miles of 42-inch pipeline in Churchill, Humboldt, Lyon, Pershing, Storey and Washoe Counties. That notice says BLM intends to be a cooperating agency and Reclamation asked to be a Section 106 consulting party. On this precedent, BLM would likely adopt the FERC document for the pipeline, keeping FMDP's NEPA separate unless BLM chooses to combine. Whether the 2028 project is the pipeline planned in the FMDP corridor is unverified.
