## What it is

D.C. Circuit decision (Edwards, S.J., joined by Silberman, S.J.; Brown, J., concurring in part and in the judgment) granting review of FERC's EA for Tennessee Gas's Northeast Upgrade Project on the 300 Line pipeline.

## Operative text or holding

FERC impermissibly segmented review by analyzing the Northeast Project alone when it and three other upgrades on the Eastern Leg were physically, functionally, and financially interdependent and overlapped in time. FERC did not show logical termini or substantial independent utility; separate shipping contracts for each increment did not show independent utility where the projects were financially interdependent. The court stressed timing: projects separated by more time could have independent utility. The EA's cumulative impacts analysis was also deficient.

## Quotes

> "Given the self-evident interrelatedness of the projects as well as their temporal overlap, the Commission was obliged to consider the other three other Tennessee Gas pipeline projects when it conducted its NEPA review of the Northeast Project." (753 F.3d at 1308)
> "The commercial and financial viability of a project when considered in isolation from other actions is potentially an important consideration in determining whether the substantial independent utility factor has been met." (753 F.3d at 1316)
> "Separated by more time, the projects could have utility independent of the other projects." (753 F.3d at 1318)
> "There were clear indications in the record that the improvement projects were functionally and financially interdependent, and the absence of logical termini suggests that the four projects functioned as one unified upgrade of the Eastern Leg." (753 F.3d at 1319)

## Notes

- Judge Brown joined only Part II.C (cumulative impacts) and would not have reached segmentation; Judge Silberman joined the full opinion, citing its emphasis on timing. The segmentation holding is a two-judge majority.
- Out-of-circuit for Nevada projects: persuasive only.
- The Taxpayers Watchdog four-factor test (logical termini, substantial independent utility, no foreclosure of alternatives, no irretrievable commitment of funds) is quoted at 753 F.3d at 1315; the court said the first two factors were relevant.
