## What it is

Ninth Circuit decision (Brunetti, J.) reversing a district court ruling that the Corps violated NEPA when it limited its EA for a 16.1-acre wetlands fill permit to the permitted activities rather than the whole Playa Vista development.

## Operative text or holding

The Corps may limit NEPA scope to the permitted activity where the federal and private parts of a project could exist independently. The Corps had no independent jurisdiction over the upland Phase I development (about 600 acres, 16 subject to the permit), which was proceeding without the permit, used no federal money, and was designed under state and local rules. Thomas v. Peterson does not apply where the agency lacks jurisdiction over the other action. The three development phases had independent utility, so they were not connected actions, and requiring joint analysis of undeveloped later phases would require the impractical.

## Quotes

> "The Corps’ determination of the appropriate scope of the environmental review process is entitled to deference." (222 F.3d at 1115)
> "We have upheld an agency’s decision to limit the scope of its NEPA review to the activities specifically authorized by the federal action where the private and federal portions of the project could exist independently of each other." (222 F.3d at 1116)
> "Here, by contrast, the Corps does not have independent jurisdiction over the parts of the Phase I development that do not require the filling of wetlands." (222 F.3d at 1117)
> "We use an “independent utility” test to determine whether an agency is required to consider multiple actions in a single NEPA review pursuant to the CEQ regulations." (222 F.3d at 1118)

## Notes

- Save Our Sonoran (408 F.3d at 1124) distinguished this case because the Playa Vista wetlands phase was separable from the uplands; see A-case-save-our-sonoran.
- The case applies the Corps' NEPA regulation at 33 C.F.R. pt. 325, app. B, section 7(b) (the "control and responsibility" test) and CEQ 40 C.F.R. 1508.25. The CEQ regulations have since been rescinded; the status of Appendix B was not checked in this session.
- It cites Sylvester as 884 F.2d 394 (Sylvester I), not 882 F.2d 407. See A-case-sylvester.
