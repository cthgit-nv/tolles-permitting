## What it is

NEPA section 111, added by the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023. It defines the statutory NEPA terms, including "major Federal action", "lead agency", "cooperating agency", "environmental document", and "special expertise".

## Operative text or holding

A major Federal action is an action the acting agency determines is subject to substantial Federal control and responsibility. Paragraph (10)(B) lists seven exclusions. Exclusion (i) removes a non-Federal action (I) with no or minimal Federal funding, or (II) with no or minimal Federal involvement where a Federal agency cannot control the outcome of the project. Other exclusions cover general revenue sharing, loans and financial assistance without sufficient control, certain SBA loan guarantees, enforcement actions, extraterritorial activities, and non-discretionary decisions.

## Quotes

> "means an action that the agency carrying out such action determines is subject to substantial Federal control and responsibility" (42 U.S.C. 4336e(10)(A))
> "with no or minimal Federal funding; or" (42 U.S.C. 4336e(10)(B)(i)(I))
> "with no or minimal Federal involvement where a Federal agency cannot control the outcome of the project" (42 U.S.C. 4336e(10)(B)(i)(II))
> "activities or decisions that are non-discretionary and made in accordance with the agency's statutory authority" (42 U.S.C. 4336e(10)(B)(vii))

## Notes

- (10)(B)(i) is framed as two alternatives joined by "or": minimal funding, or minimal involvement where the agency cannot control the outcome. The funding prong (I) does not on its face carry the "cannot control the outcome" qualifier.
- The uscode.house.gov rendering of (10)(B)(iv) contains footnote artifacts ("section 7(a) or (b) and 1 of the Small Business Act ( U.S.C. 636(a)),2"). These are the Code's editorial footnote markers, not our transcription errors; the saved source keeps them as rendered.
