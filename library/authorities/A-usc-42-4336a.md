## What it is

NEPA section 107, added by the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023 (Pub. L. 118-5, div. C, title III, sec. 321(b)). It sets the procedural rules for lead, joint lead, and cooperating agencies, a single environmental document, page limits, sponsor-prepared documents, and completion deadlines.

## Operative text or holding

- (a): participating Federal agencies designate a lead agency by letter or memorandum using five listed factors; State, Tribal, or local agencies may be joint leads; the lead agency may designate cooperating agencies with jurisdiction by law or special expertise; CEQ designates a lead if agencies cannot agree within 45 days of a request.
- (b): one environmental document, to the extent practicable, where more than one Federal agency must act.
- (e): EIS limit 150 pages (300 pages for extraordinary complexity); EA limit 75 pages; citations and appendices excluded.
- (f): the lead agency must prescribe procedures letting a project sponsor prepare an EA or EIS under agency supervision; the agency independently evaluates the document and takes responsibility for its contents.
- (g): EIS within 2 years and EA within 1 year of the sooner of three trigger dates (determination that the document is required, notice that a right-of-way application is complete, or notice of intent). The lead agency may extend in consultation with the applicant. A project sponsor may petition a court over a missed deadline; the court sets a schedule not exceeding 90 days unless a longer period is necessary.
- (h): annual report to House Natural Resources and Senate EPW on missed deadlines.

## Quotes

> "an environmental impact statement shall not exceed 150 pages, not including any citations or appendices" (42 U.S.C. 4336a(e)(1)(A))
> "An environmental assessment shall not exceed 75 pages, not including any citations or appendices." (42 U.S.C. 4336a(e)(2))
> "A lead agency shall prescribe procedures to allow a project sponsor to prepare an environmental assessment or an environmental impact statement under the supervision of the agency." (42 U.S.C. 4336a(f))
> "the date on which such agency notifies the applicant that the application to establish a right-of-way for such action is complete" (42 U.S.C. 4336a(g)(1)(A)(ii))

## Notes

- For a right-of-way project, the completeness notice is one of the three trigger dates, so the 1-year EA and 2-year EIS clocks can start at application completeness, earlier than a notice of intent.
- 42 U.S.C. 4336f (NEPA section 112, see A-usc-42-nepa-112) shortens these deadlines for sponsors that pay the opt-in fee, "Notwithstanding section 4336a(g)(1)".
- Interior implements 4336a(f) at 43 CFR 46.107 (see A-cfr-43-46).
