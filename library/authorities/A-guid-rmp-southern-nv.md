## What it is

The land use plan that governs BLM decisions in the Las Vegas and Pahrump Field Offices (Southern Nevada District) is the 1998 Las Vegas RMP, as amended. A full revision was started in 2010, and a Draft EIS was published in 2014 (79 FR 61334). BLM terminated the revision in May 2019 and chose case-by-case amendments instead. `decided` uses the termination date. `weight: binding` reflects FLPMA's conformance requirement (43 U.S.C. 1732(a); 43 CFR 1610.5-3). Neither provision was read in this session.

## Operative text or holding

- The 1998 Las Vegas RMP (as amended) remains the governing plan. The revision was terminated in favor of "smaller, focused RMP amendments".
- Recent use confirms it is still current. BLM's July 2026 Mosey Solar NOI proposes to amend "the existing Las Vegas RMP" to realign two undeveloped utility corridors: a Section 368 corridor (Segment 224-225) and an RMP-designated utility corridor established by the 1998 RMP. The Greenlink West ROD (2024) amended "the 1998 ROD for the Approved Las Vegas RMP and Final EIS" along with the Carson City and Tonopah plans.
- The Mosey NOI also states that the project was determined to be a FAST-41 covered project on January 17, 2025 (see A-usc-42-4370m).

## Quotes

> "The preparation of an Environmental Impact Statement (EIS) for the Resource Management Plan (RMP) Revision for the Las Vegas and Pahrump Field Offices is hereby terminated." (84 FR 20657)
> "the BLM decided to continue management of the public lands in the Las Vegas and Pahrump planning area (Southern Nevada District Office) under the existing RMP (as amended) and to consider additional plan amendments on a case-by-case basis, as needed." (84 FR 20657)
> "Southern Nevada District utility corridor, established by the 1998 Las Vegas RMP (referred to as the RMP-designated utility corridor), intersects the center of the Project site." (FR Doc. 2026-15270, Mosey Solar NOI)

## Treatment

2026-09-24: no Notice of Intent to revise the Las Vegas RMP after 2019 was found in the Federal Register API searches run in this session. The searches were not exhaustive; no BLM planning page was searched. The 2026 Mosey NOI treats the 1998 plan as current.

## Notes

- Jean: the ROW must conform to the 1998 Las Vegas RMP as amended. The plan's ROW exclusion and avoidance areas, utility corridor map, desert tortoise ACEC boundaries and visual resource classes were not read. Check them against the substation, line, road and fiber alignment.
- Siting outside a designated corridor, or in an avoidance area, can require a plan amendment, as with Mosey and Purple Sage.
- The 2024 Western Solar Plan amended RMPs across the West. A BLM press page menu referred to it, but its effect on the Las Vegas RMP was not researched.
