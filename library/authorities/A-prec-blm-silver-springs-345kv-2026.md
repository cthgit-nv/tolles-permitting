## What it is

A 2026 EA and FONSI for a new NV Energy ROW: a 160-foot permanent ROW and a 300-foot temporary construction ROW for a 345-kV transmission line fold connecting to the Greenlink West Project and an anticipated Nighthawk substation, plus access and maintenance roads, near Silver Springs in Lyon County. The project area is about 540.6 acres (387.9 on BLM land). Level of review reached: EA with FONSI.

## Operative text or holding

- Office: Carson City District, Sierra Front Field Office.
- Year: DR dated May 2026 (documents dated 2026-05-28). The Register shows decision and FONSI date 6/12/2026.
- Process under the 2025-2026 procedures: posted to ePlanning on April 13, 2026, with no public comment period, and decided about six weeks later. The FONSI is organized by "degree of effects" and "potentially affected environment", matching the DOI Handbook's significance format.
- What it shows the office accepts: for a new short transmission connection on largely undisturbed BLM land next to a new substation, the district chose an EA rather than a CX, even after BLM adopted the TVA and RUS transmission CEs in 2025.

## Quotes

> "The decision will grant NV Energy a 160-foot-wide permanent right-of-way (ROW) and 300-foot-wide temporary construction ROW to construct, operate, and maintain a 345-kV transmission line fold to connect to the proposed Greenlink West Project and the anticipated Nighthawk substation" (Decision Record p. 1)
> "The Project was posted to ePlanning on April 13, 2026. No public comment period or notification process occurred." (Decision Record p. 2)
> "approximately 540.6 acres, comprised of 387.9 acres of BLM-administered land and 152.7 acres of private land in Lyon County." (FONSI p. 1)

It suggests the district's default for new transmission ROWs is still an EA, and that the EA can be done without a public comment period. Whether the Stillwater FO (Hazen) follows the same practice is not shown.
- This decision ties into Greenlink West (DOI-BLM-NV-0000-2022-0004-EIS), an EIS-level project. The fold was analyzed in its own EA, not as part of the Greenlink EIS.
