## What it is

The Board affirmed a BLM Ely District (Nevada) record of decision, based on an EIS, approving the Lincoln County Land Act Groundwater Development and Utility Right-of-Way Project: a buried water pipeline, power and ancillary facilities. It rejected claims about hard look, supplementation, alternatives, connected actions, cumulative effects and mitigation, and denied a hearing request. It is a Nevada linear-utility ROW decision.

## Operative text or holding

- Organizational standing: the interests must be germane to the group's purpose, and at least one member must have an interest that is or may be adversely affected.
- Connected actions: the water pipeline and a separately approved power plant (the Toquop Energy Project) that would share part of the pipeline route were not connected actions. They were studied in separate EISs, and each would proceed whether or not the other did. Routing two similar ROWs into one corridor to avoid unnecessary new impacts does not make them connected. The Board adopted BLM's phrase "smart land use planning".
- No evidentiary hearing where the dispute is scientific disagreement about data and methodology.

## Quotes

> "In order to show organizational standing, an appellant group must show that the interests it seeks to protect are germane to its organizational purposes" (181 IBLA at 325, headnote 1)
> "the potential environmental impacts of the respective projects have been studied in separate EISs and the actions are independent, i.e., where either action will proceed whether the other action proceeds or not." (181 IBLA at 327, headnote 6)
> "Routing the pipeline through the Tule Desert to the TEP site under the Proposed Action using the same corridor" (181 IBLA at 358)
> "does not establish that they are connected actions." (181 IBLA at 358)
> "similar rights-of-way into the same corridor to avoid future unnecessary environmental impacts does not" (181 IBLA at 358, quoting BLM; sentence continues on 359)
