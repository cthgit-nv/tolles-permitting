## What it is

Interior's interim final rule, effective July 3, 2025, rescinding most of 43 CFR Part 46 and moving the rest of its NEPA procedures to a DOI NEPA Handbook, after CEQ removed its regulations.

## Operative text or holding

Revised Part 46 to keep only 46.105, 46.107, 46.150, 46.205, 46.210, and 46.215, with targeted updates; all other sections were removed, including 46.220 (lead agencies) and 46.225 (cooperating agencies). Comments were due August 4, 2025. Adopted with changes by the final rule at 91 FR 8738 (Feb. 24, 2026), which reinstated 46.220 and 46.225.

## Quotes

> "The interim final rule is effective July 3, 2025." (90 FR 29498)
> "All other provisions will be removed from 43 CFR part 46." (90 FR 29499)
> "Specifically, DOI intends to retain and make limited updates to provisions relating to emergency responses" (90 FR 29499)
