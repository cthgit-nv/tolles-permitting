## What it is

BLM's EA-level approval of a 450-mile buried fiber optic line from Reno to Las Vegas (327 miles on BLM land), with nine in-line amplifier buildings, placed mostly in existing highway and county road rights-of-way, including along U.S. 50 through Churchill County. It used a 30-year long-term ROW and a 3-year construction ROW. Reclamation was a cooperating agency and adopted the BLM EA for the Reclamation-land crossings. Level of review reached: EA with FONSI.

## Operative text or holding

- Office: Carson City District. The DR letterhead reads Stillwater Field Office, and the DR was signed by the Carson City District Manager.
- Year: DR and FONSI signed 3/6/2024. EA public comment ran 12/11/2023 to 1/10/2024. The DR was corrected 6/10/2024 to say the long-term grant is 30 years, not 50.
- Section 106 was handled by NEPA substitution (36 C.F.R. 800.8(c)). ESA formal consultation covered desert tortoise.
- Reclamation's LBAO adopted the BLM EA (record dated 30-APR-2024), citing 40 C.F.R. 1500.4(p) and 43 C.F.R. 46.120.
- What it shows the office accepts: even a linear utility that stays within existing road ROWs got an EA, not a CX, because of its length and scale. It is also a concrete example of Reclamation adopting a BLM EA in the Newlands area.

## Notes

- Citation error in the agency's own document: the DR header reads "DOI-BLM-NV-C000-2023-0002-EA", but the Register, the FONSI context, and Reclamation's record use "-0003-EA". Use 0003 and note the discrepancy.
- Hazen relevance: this is the nearest recent Carson City District and LBAO precedent for a linear utility crossing both BLM and Newlands Project land in Churchill County. The two agencies used one EA, with BLM as lead and Reclamation adopting. That is a model for coordinating Hazen Application 1 (Reclamation) and Application 2 (BLM), if both agencies agree. Reclamation's adoption relied on 43 C.F.R. 46.120, which the 2025-2026 rulemaking removed. The current basis for adopting another agency's EA would be the DOI NEPA Handbook, sec. 3.1 (Reliance on existing environmental documents). Not read in detail this session.
- Full EA: "Reno to Las Vegas Fiber Optic Project Plan -EA Final-Clean_508.pdf" on the Register Documents page. Not downloaded.
