## What it is

ESA section 7. Each federal agency, in consultation with FWS (or NMFS), must ensure that any action it authorizes, funds or carries out is not likely to jeopardize a listed species or destroy or adversely modify designated critical habitat. The statute sets the consultation clock and the biological opinion and incidental take statement mechanism. `decided` is the ESA enactment date (Pub. L. 93-205), not verified in this session.

## Operative text or holding

- (a)(2): the substantive duty to avoid jeopardy and adverse modification, using the best scientific and commercial data available.
- (a)(3): a prospective permit or license applicant may ask the agency to start early consultation.
- (b)(1): formal consultation must conclude within 90 days of initiation, or within a mutually agreed period. For an applicant's action, an extension to 150 days requires a written statement to the applicant, and anything past 150 days requires the applicant's consent.
- (b)(3)(A): the biological opinion must set out reasonable and prudent alternatives if jeopardy is found.
- (b)(4): if the action will not violate (a)(2) and incidental take will not either, FWS issues a written statement specifying the impact of the take, reasonable and prudent measures, and terms and conditions binding on the agency or the applicant or both.
- (c)(1): the agency asks FWS whether listed or proposed species may be present. If so, it prepares a biological assessment within 180 days, which may be done as part of NEPA compliance.
- (d): after consultation begins, neither the agency nor the applicant may make an irreversible or irretrievable commitment of resources that forecloses reasonable and prudent alternatives.

## Quotes

> "Each Federal agency shall, in consultation with and with the assistance of the Secretary, insure that any action authorized, funded, or carried out by such agency (hereinafter in this section referred to as an "agency action") is not likely to jeopardize the continued existence of any endangered species or threatened species" (16 U.S.C. 1536(a)(2))
> "Consultation under subsection (a)(2) with respect to any agency action shall be concluded within the 90-day period beginning on the date on which initiated" (16 U.S.C. 1536(b)(1)(A))
> "sets forth the terms and conditions (including, but not limited to, reporting requirements) that must be complied with by the Federal agency or applicant (if any), or both" (16 U.S.C. 1536(b)(4)(iv))
> "the Federal agency and the permit or license applicant shall not make any irreversible or irretrievable commitment of resources with respect to the agency action" (16 U.S.C. 1536(d))

The FWS and NMFS proposed revisions to the section 7 regulations (50 CFR part 402) at 90 FR 52600 (Nov. 21, 2025); comments closed Dec. 22, 2025. No final rule was found. Separately, 91 FR 43300 (July 14, 2026) rescinds the regulatory definition of "harm" in 50 CFR parts 17 and 222, effective September 14, 2026. That change bears on section 9 take and on the scope of incidental take statements, not on the section 7(a)(2) standard itself.
