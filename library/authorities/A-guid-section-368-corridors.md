## What it is

Section 368 of EPAct 2005 directed the designation of multi-agency energy corridors on federal land in the 11 western states. BLM's January 2009 ROD amended 92 RMPs to designate about 5,000 miles of corridors. A settlement led to regional reviews (final report April 2022). In December 2023 BLM noticed an RMP amendment/EIS to revise seven corridors and add one. `decided` uses the NOI date.

## Operative text or holding

- The seven designated corridors noticed for revision are 16-104, 18-23, 27-41, 30-52, 81-213, 113-114 and 138-143, plus a new Wamsutter-Powder Rim corridor. For 27-41, Alternative A would extend the corridor east to Laughlin, Nevada. The NOI lists 19 RMPs to be amended, including the Las Vegas RMP and the Carson City Field Office Consolidated RMP. The NOI does not say which corridor touches which RMP.
- Jean / Ivanpah: a designated Section 368 corridor, Segment 224-225, "North Pahrump/U.S. 95 to Las Vegas/Ivanpah Valley", is identified in BLM's 2024 Purple Sage notice. The same segment is named in the 2026 Mosey Solar NOI; see A-guid-rmp-southern-nv. Corridor 224-225 is not among the seven noticed for revision in 2023.
- Hazen / Lahontan Valley: the FY2023 NDAA confirms a "designated West-Wide Energy Corridor" adjacent to the B-16 Range with an existing transmission line along B-16's western boundary; see A-usc-frtc-withdrawal. Its corridor number was not identified.
- Designation authorizes no ground disturbance; each project still needs its own ROW decision and NEPA review (NOI, Summary of Expected Impacts).

## Treatment

2026-09-24: no Draft EIS, Final EIS or ROD for the 2023 Section 368 revision effort was found in a Federal Register API search for "Section 368 Energy Corridor" (results through July 2026). Its status after scoping is unknown. The Greenlink West ROD (FR Doc. 2024-20864) modified "certain Section 368 corridors" by amending the Carson City, Tonopah and Las Vegas RMPs and the 2009 ROD. The segments it changed were not identified.
2026-09-24: the West-wide Energy Corridor Information Center (corridoreis.anl.gov) corridor abstracts, including 27-225 and 224-225, returned HTTP 403 to both curl and WebFetch. Their text was not read.

## Notes

- Jean: a web search snippet of the Argonne abstract for corridor 27-225 said it runs to the intersection of corridors 224-225 and 225-231 "in the southern part of Clark County and south of Jean, NV". That is a search engine summary, not a primary text read; verify before use. If confirmed, Jean sits at or near a junction of designated corridors. That matters for siting and for the 2009 ROD interagency operating procedures, which were not read.
- Hazen: see A-usc-frtc-withdrawal. Sharing the FMDP corridor with the Great Basin Gas Transmission pipeline and the NV Energy line may or may not track a designated Section 368 corridor. Confirm against the Carson City RMP corridor maps (see A-guid-rmp-carson-city).
- Siting inside a designated corridor usually eases RMP conformance. Siting outside one can require a plan amendment, as with Greenlink North; see A-guid-rmp-carson-city.
