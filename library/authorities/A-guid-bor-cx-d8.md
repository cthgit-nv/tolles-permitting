## What it is

Reclamation's categorical exclusion for issuing or renewing 43 C.F.R. Part 429 use authorizations, including crossing agreements that provide rights-of-way. It was revised in January 2025 while it sat in 516 DM 14, and it now sits in Appendix 2 of the February 2026 DOI NEPA Handbook (516 DM 1), Section 14.5, paragraph D(8). The text did not change in the move.

## Operative text or holding

The CE covers a Part 429 use authorization (crossing agreements included) where at least one of four prongs applies: (a) impacts are expected to be minor and localized; (b) the action does not lead to a major public or private action; (c) the only result is an administrative or financial practice or change; or (d) the level of use or impacts to resources is not increased. The prongs are joined by "one or more", so one prong is enough. The asterisk means the CE must be documented, with an extraordinary circumstances review under 43 C.F.R. 46.215. Reclamation explained in 2025 that "minor and localized" is judged by physical size, surrounding land use, and ground disturbance on previously undisturbed land. The 2025 revision also moved the old D(10) crossing-agreement CE into D(8) (D(10) is now "Reserved"). Under Appendix 2, any DOI bureau may use another bureau's CE.

## Quotes

> "Issuance or renewal of use authorizations (as defined in 43 CFR 429.2, including crossing agreements which provide rights-of-way) that authorize use of Reclamation land, facilities, or waterbodies where one or more of the following apply: (a) impacts of the action are expected to be minor and localized; (b) the action does not lead to a major public or private action; (c) the only result of the authorization will be to implement an administrative or financial practice or change; or (d) the level of use or impacts to resources is not increased." (DOI NEPA Handbook, App. 2, 14.5(D)(8), handbook p. 94)
> "the following Bureau actions are designated documented categorical exclusions unless one or more of the extraordinary circumstances applies" (App. 2, 14.5, handbook p. 92)
> "Citations to these CEs should follow this format: DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, [SECTION NUMBER], [PARAGRAPH NUMBER OR LETTER]." (App. 2 introduction, handbook p. 53)
> "the DOI NEPA procedures provide that any bureau can use another bureau’s CE" (App. 2 introduction, handbook p. 53)
> "Under D8, minor and localized considerations would include things such as physical size, surrounding land use, and extent of potential ground disturbance on previously undisturbed land." (90 FR 2735, response to comments)
> "The revised categorical exclusions are incorporated into Reclamation's NEPA procedures, located at Chapter 14 of Part 516 of the Departmental Manual (516 DM 14), effective January 13, 2025." (90 FR 2735, DATES)

## Notes

- Memo citation check: the memo cites "CX 14.5D(8), revised eff. Jan. 13, 2025, DOI NEPA Handbook App. 2". The substance is right: four prongs joined by "one or more", and January 13, 2025 is the effective date of the revision. The Handbook's own citation format is "DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, 14.5(D)(8)". The January 2025 revision was made to 516 DM 14, and the text moved into the Handbook in 2025-2026. Say both if the date is cited.
- Before 2025, crossings were usually covered by old 516 DM 14.5 D(10) ("Issuance of permits, licenses, easements, and crossing agreements which provide right-of-way over Bureau lands where the action does not allow for or lead to a major public or private action"). LBAO CECs from 2021-2023 cite D(10). D(10) is now Reserved.
- Prong (b) ("does not lead to a major public or private action") may be contested for a crossing that serves a large private development. Prong (a) depends on size and new disturbance. Hazen Application 1 includes a new ~2,750 ft paved road on Reclamation land, so prong (a) and extraordinary circumstances need a fact record. That is a factual question for the project file, not settled here.
- A second copy of the Handbook exists at https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/documents/2026-02/doi-handbook-nepa-procedures-concluded-final-approval-publish-final.pdf. Its D(8) text is the same (checked by search, not quoted here).
