## What it is

Second Ninth Circuit appeal ("Sylvester II," Sneed, J.) in the challenge to a Corps section 404 permit to fill about eleven acres of wetlands for a golf course at the Resort at Squaw Creek. Affirms denial of a preliminary injunction.

## Operative text or holding

The Corps could accept the applicant's legitimate project purpose (an on-site 18-hole golf course for the resort) in its CWA practicable alternatives analysis, and could consider the golf course's relationship to the resort in that analysis without that relationship "federalizing" the whole resort. The Corps properly measured the golf course's benefits by its contribution to the resort, under both the CWA and NEPA. This opinion is about alternatives and benefits; the scope-of-federal-action holding is in Sylvester I (see Notes).

## Quotes

> "Obviously, an applicant cannot define a project in order to preclude the existence of any alternative sites and thus make what is practicable appear impracticable." (882 F.2d at 409)
> "A relationship required to be considered in determining reasonable and practicable alternatives need not be of such significance as would be necessary to “federalize” the entire project." (882 F.2d at 410)
> "The EA makes plain that the Corps followed its regulations and weighed only the benefits of the golf course to the resort." (882 F.2d at 410)

## Notes

- Citation check needed on counsel's side. The well-known NEPA scope holding (the golf course does not "federalize" the resort; the "broken chain" image; each segment stands alone) is in Sylvester I, 871 F.2d 817 (9th Cir. 1989) (decided Mar. 27, 1989), which Sylvester II cites at 882 F.2d at 409 ("See 871 F.2d at 823"). Later Ninth Circuit cases (Wetlands Action Network, 222 F.3d at 1115 to 1116; Save Our Sonoran, 408 F.3d at 1121) and Border Power cite the same Sylvester I opinion as 884 F.2d 394 (also dated Mar. 27, 1989 in the Caselaw Access Project metadata). The relationship between 871 F.2d 817 and 884 F.2d 394 (likely an amended or reissued opinion) was not determined. If a document cites 882 F.2d 407 for the "federalization" or "links in a chain" rule, it probably means Sylvester I.
- The source file includes the full Sylvester I text (871 F.2d 817) as a labeled supplement, for reference only. Its chain passage is at 871 F.2d at 822, and "We do not understand how Perini’s desire to place the course in a meadow that contains wetlands can federalize the entire resort complex" follows the *823 marker. A separate card for Sylvester I may be worth creating.
