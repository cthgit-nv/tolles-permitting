## What it is

An LBAO categorical exclusion checklist approving a 5-year use authorization for NV Energy to use Reclamation land as a helicopter landing and staging area during the rebuild of three 120 kV lines (Verdi to Donner Summit). It applies the revised Reclamation CE, 516 DM 14.5 D(8), quoting the four prongs. Level of review reached: CE (documented CEC with extraordinary circumstances review).

## Operative text or holding

- Office: Reclamation, Interior Region 10, Lahontan Basin Area Office.
- Date: the signature date could not be read from the text layer. It is posted in the "CECs for LBAO in 2026" list, which is dated 01/01/2026, and its tracking number is CGB-ED-2025-157. The decided field above uses the list date and is therefore marked unverified.
- CE cited: "516 DM 14.5 D. (8)" with the full revised text. Extraordinary circumstances were screened against 43 C.F.R. 46.215 and ITA policy, with concurrences on circumstances 6 and 10.
- What it shows LBAO accepts: after the January 2025 revision, LBAO uses D(8) for Part 429 use authorizations serving utility work. It still uses the older 516 DM citation. Another 2026 LBAO CEC (Lahontan Check Measurement Bridge, CGB-ED-2026-050) uses the new form "DOI NEPA Handbook, 516 DM 1, Appendix 2, Bureau of Reclamation, 14.5 C(3)".
- Earlier LBAO practice (in the same source file): crossing and road licenses were covered by old D.10, for example the Marburg Technology 30-year road license near Fallon (LO-2022-1023) and the City of Fernley wastewater pipeline license (LO-2021-1024).

## Notes

- Relevance to Hazen Application 1: this is direct LBAO precedent that Newlands-area use authorizations for roads, pipelines and utility uses have been handled by CE (old D.10, now D(8)). All the examples found are existing or small facilities (existing road, existing pipeline, temporary staging). No LBAO CEC was found for a new paved public road of about 2,750 feet or for a bundle of new utilities across multiple section corners. For a new substation on Reclamation land in Churchill County, LBAO used an EA (Lahontan Substation Rebuild and Upgrade, EA LO-2012-1023, FONSI signed Aug. 1, 2014). See the research log.
- The Marburg license expressly anticipates future utility uses in a road license. That is a useful drafting model for bundling road and utility crossings.
