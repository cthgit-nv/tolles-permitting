## What it is

BLM rule on assignment of FLPMA right-of-way grants and leases, and on name changes.

## Operative text or holding

Assignment requires BLM approval, including changes of control such as mergers, but not transactions within the same corporate family. The assignee files an assignment application and follows the procedures for a new grant, including fees; preliminary review and public meetings are not required. BLM approves if in the public interest and may modify terms, add bonding, and raise or lower rent based on the new holder's exemption status. Only issued grants and leases are assignable; pending applications create no property rights and may not be assigned, except a preferred applicant's application under 2804.30(g) and continuation by an entity that becomes a wholly owned subsidiary.

## Quotes

> "With the BLM's approval, you may assign, in whole or in part, any right or interest in a grant or lease." (43 CFR 2807.21(a))
> "Your assignment is not recognized until the BLM approves it in writing." (43 CFR 2807.21(e))
> "pending right-of-way applications do not create any property rights or other interest and may not be assigned from one entity to another" (43 CFR 2807.21(g))
> "We may decrease rents if the new holder qualifies for an exemption (see § 2806.14) or waiver or reduction (see § 2806.15) and the previous holder did not." (43 CFR 2807.21(e))
