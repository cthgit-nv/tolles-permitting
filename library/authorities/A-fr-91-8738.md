## What it is

Interior's final rule, effective February 24, 2026, adopting the July 2025 interim final rule with changes. It produces the current 43 CFR Part 46 (see A-cfr-43-46) and keeps most procedures in the DOI NEPA Handbook.

## Operative text or holding

Adopts the IFR as final with changes; responds to comments; reinstates 46.220 (lead agencies) and 46.225 (cooperating agencies) in modified form, citing public comment and 42 U.S.C. 4336a(a) and (b). In 46.225 it uses "agency" in place of "eligible governmental entity" and makes cooperating-agency invitations discretionary for EAs. It makes minor revisions to 46.105 (adding references to NEPA and a Secretarial Order) and to 46.107(c) (dropping the phrase that limited when another agency may verify analyses). The regulatory text begins at 91 FR 8758.
