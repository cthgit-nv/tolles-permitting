# Project Denim (Jean): BLM right-of-way

> **State file. Present tense only.** Sensitivity: INTERNAL. Last updated 2026-09-24.

## What this is

A BLM FLPMA Title V right-of-way for a substation, transmission lines, an access road and
fiber optic, serving the Project Denim facilities on the Nevada Division of State Lands
leasehold at Jean (lease 7926, fully executed 2026-09-02). Applicant: Jean Data Partners
LLC, a Tolles entity.

## Where it stands

- **Pre-application.** No NEPA material found in Dropbox as of 2026-09-24.
- **Expected pathway: EA.** The footprint (facts F4) rules out BLM's adopted transmission
  CX: the substation (about 50 to 60 acres) is five to six times the roughly 10-acre limit,
  and the 1.2-mile road likely exceeds the 1 mile of new road allowed outside the ROW. The
  1.25-mile 230 kV line alone would fit, but excluding the line while reviewing the
  substation would be segmentation. Plan an applicant-prepared EA; consider the Section
  112 fee (180-day EA). The Las
  Vegas FO has recently approved a data center conversion (substation, BESS, fiber, road)
  and a 500 kV loop-in by DNA on existing EAs (`A-prec-blm-townsite-data-center-dna-2026`,
  `A-prec-blm-desertlink-sloan-canyon-dna-2026`): check whether an existing EA covers the
  Denim route. Seven County scoping applies to the leasehold facilities: they
  are non-federal and are disclosed as context.

## Critical risks

1. **Desert tortoise.** Section 7 consultation; check for a BLM Southern Nevada programmatic
   biological opinion (`Q-20260924-desert-tortoise-pathway`).
2. **Land use plan conformance and corridors.** Whether the route sits in a designated
   corridor under the governing resource management plan or Section 368.
3. **UEPA permit required.** The line is 230 kV outside an incorporated city, so it needs a
   PUCN permit (NRS 704.860(2)(a)); early notice is due the day the SF-299 is filed.
4. **Substation size in tortoise habitat.** 50 to 60 acres of new disturbance is the
   largest impact driver. Siting and mitigation decide the FONSI.
