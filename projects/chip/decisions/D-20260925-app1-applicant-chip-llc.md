# D-20260925-app1-applicant-chip-llc: CHIP LLC is the applicant of record for Application 1

## Decision

CHIP LLC (the Master Developer and owner of 49 CHIP parcels, 2,383 acres; CHIP Brain shared-facts, Land and boundary) is the applicant of record on the SF-299 and PPOD, with assignment to the CHIP GID after formation, subject to Reclamation approval. Replaces "Tolles Development" in the EPS draft. Resolves Meta comment C5 (Churchill County as applicant) and the counsel-hold bracket.

## Why

The landowner connecting its own parcels is the natural applicant and reinforces the crossings' independent purpose.

## Note on convention

The CHIP Brain's role-name rule (Master Developer, never the entity name) governs GID instruments and public material. A federal application must name the legal applicant, so the entity name appears on the SF-299 and PPOD by necessity. Keep role names elsewhere.

## Rejected

Tolles Development as applicant (EPS draft); Churchill County as applicant with later assignment (Meta C5; Aug 14 memo Q8).

## Reverse this if

Counsel advises a different entity for rent, liability or assignment reasons (Aug 14 memo Q8 remains open on the GID rent question).
