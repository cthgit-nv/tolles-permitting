# D-20260925-app1-ppod-scope: Application 1 PPOD covers the corner crossings and the Hart Lane road only

- **Project:** chip
- **Status:** Active
- **Made by / on:** Cory Hunt, 2026-09-25
- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Source:** Cory, 2026-09-25 session, responding to the PPOD 091826 review (`privileged/2026-09-25-stantec-comment-disposition.md`, `redteam/2026-09-25-ppod-091826.md`)

## Decision

1. Remove the switching-station / "transmission corridor" (~90 ac, 400-ft segments) from the Application 1 PPOD. Application 1 = four corner crossings and the Hart Lane access road segment.
2. Delete the description of the separate FMDP transmission application from the PPOD (Stantec's NEPA planner's advice; `D-20260924-hazen-two-application-structure` phasing discipline). Whether to include one neutral disclosure sentence is left to counsel (suggested text in the redline comment).
3. Remove the EO 14318 Qualifying Project request and figures. May be raised orally at pre-application after research.
4. Crossing geometry follows the Aug 20 decision (200-ft corridor over the corner, ~0.23 ac per wing, ~1.6 ac for seven wings), pending engineering confirmation that 200 ft carries the road and the 34.5 kV MV lines (`Q-20260925-crossing-width-34kv`).
5. Add the Hart Lane road: it follows the existing gravel road on Reclamation land, stops at the CHIP property line, and continues around the site perimeter on private land.

## Why

Keeps Application 1 within the D(8) prong (a) factors (size, surrounding land use, new disturbance on undisturbed land) and removes record statements that fed prong (b), 46.215(d)/(e), and segmentation.

## Rejected

Carrying the data center's distribution corridor and a railroad-parallel ROW in Application 1 (Stantec comments C21, C66, C104; EPS 9/18 draft).

## Reverse this if

- Engineering shows the MV lines cannot fit within the crossings and must run lengthwise on Reclamation land.
- Counsel advises that a written disclosure of the FMDP application is needed in the Application 1 record.
