## What this is

A single Bureau of Reclamation application under 43 C.F.R. Part 429 for (1) limited
section corner crossings on Newlands Project parcels between CHIP's private parcels,
carrying roads and utilities, and (2) a paved access road of about 2,750 feet from Hart
Lane to the westernmost CHIP section, all of its new portion on Reclamation land.
Structure: `../../decisions/D-20260924-hazen-two-application-structure.md`.

## Critical risks

1. **Segmentation from FMDP.** Opponents would argue Application 1 is the first phase of
   FMDP split off to fit a CX. The defense is independent utility: parcel access and
   utilities for CHIP regardless of transmission. Every Application 1 document must state
   that purpose on its own terms.
2. **CX fit for the road plus the full utility bundle.** The memo's CX argument rested on
   small, transverse crossings (~4.17 acres). A 2,750-foot paved road running on
   Reclamation land, plus water, wastewater, overhead, MV and fiber, makes "minor and
   localized" harder.
3. **Energy delivery framing.** The crossings carry MV power from CHIP's own gas plant, BESS
   and solar to the first data center building, with no FMDP connectivity (facts F10). This
   is the independent-utility fact: Application 1 works on day one without FMDP. Keep every
   document consistent with it, and do not describe the crossings as enabling the data
   center in a way that invites the D(8) prong (b) argument; rely on prong (a).
