## Tension to resolve first

Stantec's comments pull in two directions. Its NEPA planner says keep the PPOD narrow and silent on FMDP. Its site comments (C21, C66, C104) ask the PPOD to carry the data center's needs: a four-lane road, distribution from a switching station across Reclamation land, and a right-of-way running parallel to the railroad. EPS resolved the second set by adding a transmission corridor of about 90 acres in 400-foot segments. That addition is the largest threat to the categorical exclusion in the draft: it is longitudinal use of Reclamation land, it is 25 times the crossing footprint, and Section 6.5 argues "minor and localized" on 3.57 acres without mentioning it.

## Decisions needed from Cory before the full rewrite

1. **Switching-station corridor (about 90 acres, 400-ft segments, 34.5 kV).** What feeds the switching station? If FMDP transmission delivers to it, the corridor belongs in Application 2. If it is CHIP-internal, it can stay in CHIP but it cannot share the CX argument at 90 acres. Recommendation: remove it from this PPOD and decide its home separately.
2. **FMDP references.** Remove the Section 1 "connected-action context" language and the Section 1.1 "Related federal application" paragraph, per Stantec's planner and `D-20260924-hazen-two-application-structure`. Ask counsel whether a single neutral line disclosing other pending applications is advisable, since Reclamation will also decide Application 2.
3. **EO 14318 request.** Remove the Qualifying Project request and figures from the PPOD. It spotlights the data center as the purpose of the crossings, which feeds both the connected-action argument and CX prong (b). Raise it orally at the pre-application meeting instead, after research confirms whether any Qualifying-Project CX exists to adopt.
4. **Crossing geometry.** Cory's Aug 20 decision (200-ft corridor over the corner) gives about 0.23 acre per wing, about 1.61 acres for seven wings. The draft states 0.51 acre per wing and 3.57 acres, which matches the old ~300-ft geometry. Confirm the smaller figure.
5. **Hart Lane road and existing gravel road.** Add the ~2,750-ft paved road on Reclamation land and construction use of the existing gravel road to Application 1. Confirm how they relate: does the new road follow the gravel road, and where does it meet Hart Lane (EPS places Hart Lane about 0.7 mile northwest of Crossing 1)?
