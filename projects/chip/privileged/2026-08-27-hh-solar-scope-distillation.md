## Bottom line

Counsel agrees with Tolles' read: after Seven County, there is a stronger, "viable
argument" that solar on CHIP's private parcels is not a connected action for the
transmission line's NEPA review, provided (1) the solar facilities need no significant
federal authorizations of their own and (2) they are not presented as central to and
deeply interconnected with the federal action.

## Reasoning, in order

1. Before Seven County, scope turned on interrelatedness or independence, often framed as
   a but-for test (Wetlands Action Network; Friends of the Earth v. Coleman; Border Power).
2. Seven County: NEPA's focus is the proposed action, not separate future projects; courts
   may not use but-for causation or foreseeability to order analysis of every project that
   might follow; agencies may draw a reasonable "manageable line" and get deference.
3. Save the Sound v. FAA (D.C. Cir. July 21, 2026) reads Seven County as giving agencies
   wide latitude to define the project; interrelated projects close in time and place are
   not necessarily one project. Project-defining factors: temporal separation, substantial
   independent value, whether the activities require one another.
4. Applied: solar is built later, not concurrently; the line has independent value (other
   sources); the line does not require solar. Weakness: solar may lack independent value
   without the line and may "require" it. Presenting solar as a future, separate, not yet
   defined project may overcome that.
5. No regulatory authority, no legally relevant cause (Seven County, citing Public
   Citizen): strong support, since solar is on private land. Caveat: CWA, ESA or other
   permits for the solar could trigger their own federal review later; emphasize (if
   accurate) that these are speculative.

## Drafting instructions that follow from it

- Describe solar as a future, separate project, not yet fully defined, owned by a separate
  entity, not part of the initial development.
- State that the line is needed regardless of solar and carries other sources.
- Do not describe solar as central to or deeply interconnected with the line.
- Keep this consistent with the Aug 14 memo's instruction not to minimize solar publicly.
  Tension to manage: public announcements describe CHIP as solar plus battery plus
  geothermal plus gas. The consistent framing is shared infrastructure serving a
  portfolio, with each resource separately developed.
