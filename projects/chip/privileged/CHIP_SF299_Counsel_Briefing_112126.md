# CHIP Reclamation Application: Briefing for Counsel

PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL. ATTORNEY-CLIENT COMMUNICATION. Prepared to obtain legal advice. Nov 21, 2026.

## The project in brief

- **CHIP** (Churchill Hazen Industrial Park) is about 2,400 acres of private land near Hazen in Churchill County, owned by **CHIP LLC**. The planned uses are industrial: a data center campus, with on-site gas generation, battery storage and solar, all on private land. The end user is **Meta** (confidential; "Project Bald Eagle"). A CHIP general improvement district (GID) was approved by the County in November and is in its appeal period.
- **Why Reclamation is involved.** The CHIP parcels form a checkerboard with Newlands Project land administered by Reclamation. Several CHIP parcels touch each other only at a section corner. Roads and utilities cannot pass from one parcel to the next without crossing a corner of Reclamation land (Figure 1).
- **What we are asking for now.** A 43 CFR Part 429 use authorization for four corner crossings: seven small triangular "wings" of Reclamation land, about 1.43 ac each and about 10 ac in total (Figure 2). The crossings would carry internal park roads, water, wastewater, fiber and 34.5 kV distribution lines from on-site generation. The target review is a documented categorical exclusion.
- **What comes later, separately.**
  - **Hart Lane access road** from the north, on Reclamation land in Sec. 34, T.20N. (Figure 1, dashed line).
  - **FMDP** (Forty Mile Desert Power), a separate 345 kV transmission and access project needing BLM and Reclamation authorizations. FMDP's generation is also on private land.

## Research notes

These notes come from free public sources. None of the cases have been citator-checked; please KeyCite before relying on them.

## Categorical exclusion fit (status: open)

- **Text:** DOI NEPA Handbook App. 2, 14.5(D)(8) ([PDF](https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/document_handbooks/doi-handbook-of-nepa-procedures-concluded-508-compliant.pdf), p. 94). Its four conditions are joined by "one or more," so condition (a), "minor and localized," is enough on its own. Reclamation's reading of "minor and localized" is in 90 FR 2735 (Jan. 13, 2025).
- **Risk:** condition (b) asks whether the action "does not lead to a major public or private action." Any record language suggesting the crossings enable the private development invites the argument that (b) is not met, and feeds a connected-action theory. The redline removes the item 15 and 16 sentences for this reason.
- **Precedent:** LBAO used a categorical exclusion for an existing-road license near Fallon that allows future utilities (Marburg, LO-2022-1023, [PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=52531)). It also used one for a pipeline license (Fernley, LO-2021-1024, [PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=51649)). We found no LBAO categorical exclusion for a new paved road or a bundle of new utility crossings. LBAO used an EA for the Lahontan Substation (2014, [PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=17447)).
- **Size:** at 500 ft, the crossings total about 10.0 ac in seven triangular wings of about 1.43 ac each. The EPS draft overstated this as 20.1 ac.
- **Extraordinary circumstances:** 43 CFR 46.215 ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-46.215)). Wetlands and riverine features are mapped in two crossings. Raptor and eagle surveys are complete. Section 106 applies regardless.
- **Procedural footing:** the 2026 Interior NEPA rule (91 FR 8738, [govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-02-24/html/2026-03708.htm)) and the handbook are being challenged in litigation.

## Segmentation: crossings now, Hart Lane later (status: contested)

- **Binding and adverse:** *Thomas v. Peterson*, 753 F.2d 754 (9th Cir. 1985) ([text](https://static.case.law/f2d/753/html/0754-01.html)). A road and the timber sales it served were connected actions. This is the closest analogy against splitting the crossings from Hart Lane or from the transmission line. It applied the now-rescinded CEQ connected-actions rule, and its later treatment is questioned on other grounds.
- **Binding:** *Sovereign Inupiat / CBD v. BLM*, Nos. 23-3624, 23-3627 (9th Cir. June 13, 2025) ([PDF](https://cdn.ca9.uscourts.gov/datastore/opinions/2025/06/13/23-3627.pdf)). Upheld BLM's choice of a broad scope; the panel was receptive to segmentation concerns.
- **Supportive, persuasive only:** *Save the Sound v. FAA*, No. 24-1028 (D.C. Cir. July 21, 2026) ([PDF](https://media.cadc.uscourts.gov/opinions/docs/2026/07/24-1028-2184183.pdf)). Projects are separate when they do not overlap in time, each has substantial independent value, and neither requires the other.
- **Persuasive, the facts to avoid:** *Delaware Riverkeeper v. FERC*, 753 F.3d 1304 (D.C. Cir. 2014) ([text](https://static.case.law/f3d/753/html/1304-01.html)). Segments were held to be one project because of overlap in time and functional interdependence.
- **Deference on project definition:** *Seven County Infrastructure Coalition v. Eagle County*, 605 U.S. 168 (2025) ([PDF](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-975_m648.pdf)).
- **Facts that help:**
  - The crossings carry CHIP's own roads, water, wastewater, fiber and 34.5 kV distribution from on-site generation, with no connection to the transmission line.
  - The transmission project does not depend on the crossings.
- **Facts that hurt:**
  - The same applicant would hold both Reclamation road authorizations, serving the same park.
  - Pairing Hart Lane with the 345 kV line in one EA mixes park access into the transmission review.

## TCID and the 25-year term

- 43 CFR 429.6(a) ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/part-429)): rights-of-way longer than 25 years need approval from the "water user organizations under contract obligation for repayment." Whether TCID currently holds that status, and whether it acts by board vote, has not been verified. At a 25-year renewable term the approval step should not arise; notice to TCID under 429.6(b) still occurs.
