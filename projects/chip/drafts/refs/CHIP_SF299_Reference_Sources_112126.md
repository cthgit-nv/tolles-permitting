# CHIP Corner Crossings: Reference Sources

Public sources for the SF-299 to Reclamation's Lahontan Basin Area Office. Nov 21, 2026.

## Application rules

- **43 CFR Part 429, Use of Reclamation Land** ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/part-429)). The SF-299 is the application form for road, utility and similar systems (429.10). A $100 nonrefundable application fee is submitted with the application. Rights-of-way longer than 25 years also need approval from the water user organization responsible for project repayment (429.6(a)). Reclamation acknowledges a complete application within 30 days (429.13(a)).

## Categorical exclusion Reclamation would apply

- **DOI NEPA Handbook, Appendix 2, 14.5(D)(8)** ([PDF](https://www.doi.gov/sites/default/files/document_handbooks/doi-handbook-of-nepa-procedures-concluded-508-compliant.pdf), p. 94). Covers "issuance or renewal of use authorizations (as defined in 43 CFR 429.2, including crossing agreements which provide rights-of-way)" where impacts are "minor and localized," among other conditions. Only one condition has to apply.
- **How Reclamation reads "minor and localized"** (90 FR 2735, Jan. 13, 2025): "physical size, surrounding land use, and extent of potential ground disturbance on previously undisturbed land."
- **Extraordinary circumstances screen** that applies to any categorical exclusion: 43 CFR 46.215 ([eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-43/section-46.215)). It covers cultural resources, wetlands and floodplains, listed species, migratory birds, controversy, and related actions.

## Recent Lahontan Basin Area Office decisions

- **Marburg Technology road license, near Fallon** (LO-2022-1023) ([PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=52531)). A 30-year license for an existing gravel road, 60 ft by about 4,700 ft, on Newlands Project land. The license allows future utility use. Approved with a categorical exclusion under the crossing exclusion in effect before 2025.
- **City of Fernley wastewater pipeline license** (LO-2021-1024) ([PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=51649)). A 25-year license for a 50-ft pipeline corridor (1.025 ac). Categorical exclusion.
- **NV Energy helicopter landing zone** (CGB-ED-2025-157) ([PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=57566)). A 5-year use authorization supporting transmission line work. Applies the current 14.5(D)(8) exclusion.
- **Lahontan Check Measurement Bridge** (CGB-ED-2026-050) ([PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=57512)). A 2026 categorical exclusion checklist prepared by Larry Butcher, Acting Resources Division Manager, LBAO. Useful as a recent example of the checklist format.
- **Lahontan Substation rebuild, Churchill County** (EA LO-2012-1023, 2014) ([PDF](https://www.usbr.gov/mp/nepa/includes/documentShow.php?Doc_ID=17447)). For comparison: Reclamation prepared an environmental assessment for a new substation on its land.

Before 2025, Reclamation's crossing and right-of-way exclusion was numbered 14.5(D)(10). In January 2025 it was merged into 14.5(D)(8).
