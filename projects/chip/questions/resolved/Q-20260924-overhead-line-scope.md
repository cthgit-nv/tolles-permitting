- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Blocking?** Yes. Blocks the segmentation defense in D-20260924-hazen-two-application-structure.
- **Who can answer:** Cory / EPS

## Context

Cory listed "overhead" and "medium voltage" among the utilities the Application 1 crossings carry (2026-09-24). The Aug 14 memo described the crossing lines as internal medium-voltage collector lines, not segments of the 11-mile transmission line, and relied on that for independent utility. If any overhead line across a Reclamation crossing is FMDP transmission, Application 1 contains part of Application 2 and the split is much harder to defend. Need: voltage, owner, and function of every overhead line in Application 1.

## Answer

- **Answered:** 2026-09-24 by Cory Hunt
- **Answer:** No. The overhead lines across the Application 1 crossings are medium-voltage lines from CHIP's own gas plant, BESS and solar to CHIP load, powering the first data center building. They have no FMDP connectivity.
- **Cites:** Cory, 2026-09-24 session
