# Q-20261121-mason-road-entrance: Add a Mason Road (west) entrance to Application 1?

- **Raised:** 2026-11-21 (end user request, email Nov 12)
- **Project:** CHIP (Application 1)
- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Blocking?** No, if kept out of Application 1 (desk recommendation).
- **Who can answer:** Cory; end user's traffic consultant on feasibility; counsel on CX risk

## Context

The end user asks to add Reclamation crossings for an emergency entrance from Mason Road to the west, while its traffic consultant is still evaluating feasibility (nearby residences, stream crossing). It asks whether a route that may be dropped raises issues, whether it can be removed without re-filing, the acreage and 13(b) changes, and added survey scope.

Desk view (judgment): keep it out of Application 1. A stream crossing near residences raises extraordinary-circumstances screens (wetlands/floodplains, 43 C.F.R. 46.215, `A-cfr-43-46`) that the corner crossings otherwise avoid, adds survey scope, and an alternative that may be dropped weakens purpose and need. File it as its own Part 429 application once feasible; it does not delay the corner crossings. Hart Lane entrance goes to the Phase II EA with the 345 kV line (Cory, Nov 5 sync).
