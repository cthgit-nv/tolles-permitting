# Q-20260925-tcid-approval-process: Is TCID a 43 C.F.R. 429.6(a) "water user organization under contract obligation for repayment", and how does TCID act on approvals?

- **Raised:** 2026-09-25 by Cory ("would that have to be a Board or public approval?")
- **Project:** CHIP (Application 1), FMDP
- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Blocking?** No, while the requested term is 25 years (approval applies only to terms over 25 years).
- **Who can answer:** Counsel; LBAO realty at pre-application; TCID engineering office (775-423-2141)

## Context

43 C.F.R. 429.6(a) requires approval from water user organizations under contract obligation for repayment of the project for easements and rights-of-way over 25 years (`A-cfr-43-429`). Unverified: whether TCID currently holds a repayment obligation that makes 429.6(a) apply, and whether TCID acts by board vote at a public meeting (TCID is governed by an elected board; Nevada's Open Meeting Law, NRS 241, would then apply) or by staff. TCID's published easement policy says the District "will review the requested use and make a determination whether the use unreasonably interferes with the District's operation and maintenance" (tcid.org/easements), without naming the decision-maker. Even at 25 years, TCID is notified (429.6(b)), and the Regional Director may request TCID concurrence (429.6(c)).
