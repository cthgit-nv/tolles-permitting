## Context

The Aug 14 memo (Q7) asked whether the crossings fit CX 14.5D(8) prong (a) (impacts minor and localized) and whether to sequence roads first and utilities later. The structure has since grown: a paved road of about 2,750 feet, entirely on Reclamation land, running along rather than across Reclamation land, plus water, wastewater, overhead, MV and fiber. The transverse, minimum-footprint framing the memo relied on no longer describes the road. Also open from the memo: whether the disjunctive "one or more" reading of the CX prongs survives the argument that the crossings lead to a major private action (prong (b)). Research needed: current text and location of the CX after Interior's 2025-2026 procedure changes; Reclamation precedent CXs for roads on Newlands land; extraordinary circumstances list.

**Research 2026-09-24** (`P-reclamation-cx-d8-fit`, status open): the CX is now DOI NEPA
Handbook App. 2, 14.5(D)(8); prongs are disjunctive; the old crossing-agreement CE was
folded into D(8). Reclamation's 2025 preamble says "minor and localized" turns on physical
size, surrounding land use, and ground disturbance on previously undisturbed land. LBAO has
used revised D(8) (NV Energy helicopter landing zone) and licensed a road near Fallon that
allows future utilities (Marburg Technology Road License, old D(10)). No LBAO CEC found for
a new paved road or a bundle of new utility crossings; LBAO used an EA for the 2014
Lahontan Substation rebuild. Next: ask LBAO at pre-application whether it would document a
D(8) CEC for the road and bundle, and bring the Marburg CEC as precedent.
