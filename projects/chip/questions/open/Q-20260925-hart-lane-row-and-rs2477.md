# Q-20260925-hart-lane-row-and-rs2477: What ROW width does the three-lane Hart Lane segment need, and does the historical county road have any RS 2477 or other public-road claim?

- **Raised:** 2026-09-25 by permitting desk

- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Blocking?** Width: yes, for acreage and legal description. RS 2477: no, but it could change what Reclamation needs to authorize.
- **Who can answer:** Civil engineer (width); Churchill County Public Works and counsel (RS 2477)

## Context

The desk KMZ assumes 80 ft (~4.8 ac on Reclamation land). The Churchill County road layer in the Qualus infrastructure file includes "RS 2477 Asserted Routes" near CHIP (routes 953611 and 971217, graded dirt and asphalt), but the gravel road south of Hart Lane is not among them. RS 2477 (repealed 1976, valid existing rights preserved) granted rights-of-way only over unreserved public land; land withdrawn for the Newlands Project would not qualify unless the road predates the withdrawal.
