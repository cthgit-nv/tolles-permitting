# FMDP: Application 2 (BLM transmission and access roads)

> **State file. Present tense only.** Sensitivity: INTERNAL. Last updated 2026-09-24.

## What this is

The broader Hazen federal application: linear infrastructure only (transmission lines,
access roads and related facilities) under BLM FLPMA Title V, and Reclamation for any
segment on Reclamation land (open: `Q-20260924-fmdp-reclamation-segments`). The corridor
is planned to share alignment and access with the Great Basin Gas Transmission pipeline
(to be built) and the existing NV Energy road and transmission line. All solar, battery
storage, geothermal and other generation is on private land and outside the federal
action. Structure: `../../decisions/D-20260924-hazen-two-application-structure.md`.

Referent: FMDP is the power project. Its corridor numbers are distinct from the CHIP park
acreage and the GID district boundary (CHIP Brain).

## Where it stands

- **Pre-application.** Draft BLM PPOD (`PPOD Hazen 080426 Draft.docx`, EPS) and a Tolles
  redline exist. The Aug 14 memo put about 3.8 of the 11 line miles on BLM land and about
  1.9 miles on Reclamation land; confirm against the current route.
- **Target pathway:** EA and FONSI, applicant-prepared under BLM supervision, with the
  private generation disclosed as context only (Seven County scoping). Section 112 opt-in
  fee under consideration to fix a 180-day EA deadline (memo Q9; verify in `A-usc-42-nepa-112`).
- **EIS is unacceptable** to the business plan (memo Background 7). Every design and
  record choice is weighed against that.

## Critical risks

1. **Segmentation and phasing with Application 1.** FMDP documents must not describe the
   Reclamation crossings or the Hart Lane road as a phase of FMDP.
2. **Scope creep to private generation.** Seven County supports excluding it; Section 7
   (but-for plus reasonably certain) and Section 106 (indirect and visual effects in the
   APE) use different tests and could pull it back in (memo Q5).
3. **The pipeline is future, not existing, disturbance.** The FONSI argument built on
   paralleling existing disturbance holds only for the NV Energy road and line.
   See `Q-20260924-gbgt-pipeline-status`.
4. **Military mission conflict** (NAS Fallon, FRTC). See `Q-20260924-dod-siting-clearinghouse`.
5. **Interior NEPA procedures challenge.** If vacated, the scope position rests on the
   statute and Seven County (memo Q6). Watchlist item.
