## Context

The Aug 14 memo put about 1.9 miles of line on Reclamation land and an overhead span of the Hazen Drain (TCID-operated) in the corridor EA. FLPMA Title V does not reach Reclamation land, so if those segments remain on the route, Application 2 is a two-agency action (BLM and Reclamation) even though Application 1 is Reclamation only. That affects lead agency, cooperating agency, and whether Reclamation ends up reviewing two Hazen actions, which invites the segmentation argument. Need: the current route and which segments cross Reclamation land.
