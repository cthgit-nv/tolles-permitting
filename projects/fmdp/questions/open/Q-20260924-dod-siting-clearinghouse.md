## Context

Hazen is in the NAS Fallon area. Energy projects and tall structures near military training airspace are reviewed by the DoD Siting Clearinghouse. Transmission structures, a gas plant stack, and any tall data center or cooling structures may be in scope, as may FAA Form 7460-1 notice (14 C.F.R. Part 77). Early informal review is usually cheaper than a formal objection.
