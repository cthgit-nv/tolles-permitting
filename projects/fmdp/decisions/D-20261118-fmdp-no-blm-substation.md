# D-20261118-fmdp-no-blm-substation: No substation on BLM land; transmission line is about 14 miles

- **Project:** fmdp
- **Status:** Active
- **Made by / on:** Cory Hunt, 2026-11-18
- **Sensitivity:** INTERNAL
- **Source:** Cory, 2026-11-18 chat, answering the conflict between the EPS Nov 12 proposal (12 ac substation on BLM land, 14 mi line) and the Aug 14 memo (no substation on federal land, 11 mi line)

## Decision

Application 2 includes no substation on BLM land. The line is about 14 miles for scoping, and may move up or down somewhat.

## Why

All substations are on private land (Aug 14 memo; SF-299 narrative item 20). Keeping them off federal land keeps the federal footprint linear.

## Rejected

The EPS Nov 12 scope with a 12 ac BLM substation at the northeast terminus. The 11 mi figure from the Aug 14 memo.

## Reverse this if

Final layout puts a collector station or substation on federal land.
