## G1. Solar on private land and NEPA scope (Hazen)

**Question.** Can the NEPA review for the Hazen transmission line and access roads exclude
solar generation that will be built later on CHIP's private parcels? What conditions
matter, and what is the main weakness?

**Reference answer.** Yes, there is a viable argument after Seven County (605 U.S. 168):
NEPA's focus is the proposed action; agencies get deference to draw a reasonable line;
an agency with no authority over the private project is not its legally relevant cause
(Public Citizen). Conditions: the solar needs no significant federal authorizations of its
own, and it is not presented as central to or deeply interconnected with the federal
action. Helpful facts: built later, separate owner, the line carries other sources and is
needed regardless. Main weakness: the solar may lack independent value without the line
and may "require" it. Caveat: CWA or ESA permits for the solar could trigger their own
review. Also do not minimize solar in the public record; use the shared-infrastructure
framing.
*Source: H&H memo Aug 27 2026; Aug 14 risk memo Q1.*

## G3. Existing vs future disturbance (Hazen)

**Question.** Can the FMDP FONSI rely on the corridor paralleling existing disturbance?

**Reference answer.** Only as to the existing NV Energy road and transmission line. The
Great Basin Gas Transmission pipeline is yet to be built; it is a reasonably foreseeable
future action for cumulative effects, not existing disturbance. Documents that call it
existing need correcting.
*Source: Cory, 2026-09-24; fmdp facts F2.*

## G4. BLM categorical exclusion for new corner crossings (Hazen)

**Question.** Is there a BLM categorical exclusion that covers new corner-crossing ROWs on
BLM land?

**Reference answer.** Tolles found none as of Aug 14 2026: the realty CXs cover renewals,
assignments, existing facilities, and grants wholly within compatibly developed ROWs.
BLM crossings ride in the EA unless counsel or research finds otherwise. (Under the
current structure the Hazen crossings are Reclamation, not BLM.)
*Source: Aug 14 risk memo Q7; verify against A-guid-blm-realty-cx.*

## G5. Pathway for Denim

**Question.** What NEPA pathway should Denim expect for a BLM ROW with a substation,
transmission lines, access road and fiber, and what are the two issues most likely to
drive schedule?

**Reference answer.** An EA is the realistic expectation because a substation is a
surface footprint on BLM land; a CX is unlikely for new facilities. Schedule drivers:
desert tortoise Section 7 consultation (check for a programmatic opinion) and land use
plan and corridor conformance. Also check whether NV Energy will own the substation.
*Source: desk analysis 2026-09-24; to be confirmed by counsel. Score on elements.*
